# Lore (Planes)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Planes) covers the nature, inhabitants, and hazards of other planes and planar boundaries.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Planes) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Lore (Arcana)](lore-arcana.md) checks to identify planar magical phenomena.
