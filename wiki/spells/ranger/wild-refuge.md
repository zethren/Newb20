# Wild Refuge

| Spell detail | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 3rd |
| School | Illusion |
| Class | Ranger |
| Casting Time | 1 minute |
| Range | 30 feet |
| Components | V, S, a leaf |
| Duration | 8 hours |
| Attack | None |
| Save | None |
| Spell Resistance | No |

## Effect

Choose up to six willing creatures within range in a natural area. The spell overlays their resting place, in a space no larger than a 20-foot-radius area, with the appearance and ordinary ambient sounds of the surroundings. Creatures outside the area have disadvantage on Perception checks to notice the group unless they already know its location. Touching a concealed creature or searching the area reveals the illusion.
