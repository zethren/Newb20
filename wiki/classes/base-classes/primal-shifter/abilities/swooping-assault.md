# Swooping Assault

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raven form), Tier 5

Once per turn while flying, if you move at least 20 feet in a straight line and descend at least 10 feet toward a creature, your next melee natural weapon attack against it before the end of the turn gains +2 AB and deals +2d6 damage. On a hit, the target must succeed at a MAB check against your MAD or fall Prone.
