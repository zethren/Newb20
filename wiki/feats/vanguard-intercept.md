# Vanguard Intercept

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13; BAB +2.

You watch your ally's flank like a hawk, physically throwing your body into the path of incoming danger to absorb a blow meant for a friend.

**Action:** Reaction, in response to an enemy launching a physical melee or ranged attack against an adjacent ally.

**Check:** Roll `d20 + MAB` against the attacking enemy's MAD.

**Success:** You and your ally instantly swap 5-foot grid squares, and the incoming enemy attack roll is re-routed to resolve against your defenses, including your full armor and shield values.

**Failure:** You do not swap squares or redirect the attack; it resolves against its original target.

**Required for:** None.
