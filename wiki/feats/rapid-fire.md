# Rapid Fire

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13; BAB +1; proficiency with the ranged weapon used.

When you make a Full Attack Action with a ranged weapon, you can make one additional attack with that weapon at your highest BAB. You take a -2 penalty on all attack rolls made during that Full Attack Action. Each attack requires its own ammunition; reload weapons as required by their rules.

**Required for:** [Improved Rapid Fire](improved-rapid-fire.md).
