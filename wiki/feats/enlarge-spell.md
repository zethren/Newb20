# Enlarge Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +1; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** Double the spell's listed range. This does not affect spells with a range of Personal or Touch.

**Required for:** None.
