# Sneak Attack

Sneak Attack adds precision damage to qualifying attacks and can trigger effects from strike talents.

## 1. Sneak Attack Eligibility
* A successful attack against a target that is **Flanked** or **Flat-Footed** qualifies for Sneak Attack.
* A character adds their Sneak Attack damage to every qualifying hit made with their designated **Primary Weapon**. This includes iterative attacks.
* A qualifying hit made with a secondary or off-hand weapon can trigger strike-talent effects if it deals at least 1 damage after Damage Reduction, but does not deal Sneak Attack damage dice.

## 2. Primary Weapon Restriction
Sneak Attack damage dice apply only to attacks made with the character's designated Primary Weapon. Off-hand attacks, secondary natural attacks, and automated bonus strikes such as *Rend* do not add Sneak Attack damage dice.

## 3. Secondary Attack Talents
Secondary and off-hand attacks can still check whether the target is Flanked or Flat-Footed and apply strike-talent effects that trigger on a qualifying hit, provided the attack deals at least 1 damage after Damage Reduction. For example, [Crippling Strike and Bleeding Strike](../classes/base-classes/rogue/talents.md#debilitating-strike-talents) can apply without adding Sneak Attack damage dice.
