# Kitsune Tail Feats (Draft)

Kitsune begin with one tail. Each time a Kitsune selects this feat, they gain one additional tail and choose one distinct Tail Gift. A Kitsune can have up to nine tails total, including the original tail. These feats are optional and do not count against the ancestry's 10-RP budget.

## Additional Kitsune Tail

**Prerequisite:** Kitsune heritage.

**Benefit:** You gain one additional tail and choose one Tail Gift below. You can select this feat multiple times, up to a maximum of nine total tails. A Tail Gift can be selected only once per character, except Spell Recall, which can be selected once for each eligible spell level.

### Tail Gifts

- **Ability Gift:** Gain a +1 Supernatural bonus to one ability score. You cannot choose the same ability score for another Ability Gift.
- **Skill Gift:** Gain a +2 Supernatural bonus to one skill. You cannot choose the same skill for another Skill Gift.
- **Spell Recall:** Once per Long Rest, regain one expended spell slot of a chosen level. You must be able to cast spells of that level, and you can select Spell Recall only once per spell level. The maximum spell level available for this gift is provisionally one-half your total number of tails, rounded down, to a maximum of 4th level. This gift currently applies only to classes that expend spell slots; Sorcery Point recovery is not included.

Spell Recall's maximum level, interaction with Sorcery Points, and balance with other spell-recovery effects remain open.
