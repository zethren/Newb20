# False Double

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raccoondog form), Tier 5

**Action:** Reaction, when an attack hits you; once per encounter.

Make a MAB check against the attacker's MAD. On a success, the attack misses. You can then move up to half your base land speed without provoking an attack of opportunity from that attacker. The movement must end in an unoccupied space.
