# Guardian's Intercept

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Bear form), Tier 2

**Action:** Reaction.

When an enemy initiates a physical attack or Charge against an ally adjacent to the Bear, the Bear can intervene. The Bear makes a defensive MAB check against the enemy's attack roll. On a success, the enemy must redirect the attack against the Bear, including the Bear's Natural Armor and damage reduction.
