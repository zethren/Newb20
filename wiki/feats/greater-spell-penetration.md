# Greater Spell Penetration

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Improved Spell Penetration](improved-spell-penetration.md); caster level 8.

**Benefit:** Gain an additional +2 bonus on caster-level checks to overcome spell resistance, for a total bonus of +6.

**Required for:** None.
