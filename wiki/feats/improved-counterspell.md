# Improved Counterspell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Benefit:** When you use a Dispel spell to counter another spell, you gain a +4 bonus on the Dispel check. This bonus can exceed any caster-level bonus cap imposed by the Dispel spell; see [Counterspell](../spells.md#counterspell).

**Required for:** None.
