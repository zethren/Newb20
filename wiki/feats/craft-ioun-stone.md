# Craft Ioun Stone

**Type:** Crafting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Craft Wondrous Item](craft-wondrous-item.md); ability to cast 6th-level spells; Craft 12 ranks.

You can create fully powered Legendary Ioun Stones using the [Magic Item Enchantment rules](../game-mechanics/magic-item-enchantments.md). A stone's persistent effect costs 60 Enchantment Points.

**Required for:** None.
