# Tiefling

A Tiefling combines a planar heritage with a base ancestry. It retains that ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Tiefling heritage replaces the ancestry's other special traits.

Choose one heritage:

- **Infernal:** Energy Resistance 5 against fire.
- **Abyssal:** Energy Resistance 5 against cold. This heritage does not grant acid resistance.

Every Tiefling also gains:

- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft.
- **Prehensile Tail:** The [Tiefling Tail Feats](../feats/tiefling-tail-feats.md) allow the tail to handle items and, with further training, wield a weapon or shield.

Energy Resistance follows the shared [Energy Resistance rule](../game-mechanics.md#energy-resistance).

The heritage also grants the following fixed Racial skill bonuses. The selected traits replace the base ancestry's other special traits.

| Base ancestry | Fixed skill bonuses |
| --- | --- |
| Human | +2 Persuasion, +2 Investigation |
| Elf | +2 Perception, +2 Stealth, +2 Lore (Arcana) |
| Dwarf | +2 Craft, +2 Athletics, +2 Perception |
| Half-Orc | +2 Persuasion |
| Halfling | +2 Acrobatics, +2 Stealth |
| Half-Elf | +2 Perception, +2 Persuasion |
| Gnome | None |
