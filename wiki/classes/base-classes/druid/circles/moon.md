# Circle of the Moon

**Class:** Druid specialization

The Circle of the Moon develops Wild Shape through greater variety in animal forms and more fluid shifting, then complements transformation with lunar magic.

| Druid level | Feature |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Moon-Touched Forms |
| 6 | Fluid Shifting |
| 10 | Moonbeam |
| 14 | True Form |
| 18 | Lunar Mastery |
| 20 | Archdruid |

## Moon-Touched Forms

At 1st level, your Wild Shape can take forms with a climb or swim speed, in addition to its ordinary land-animal forms. At 6th level, you can also take forms with a Fly speed. You remain subject to the normal duration and uses of Wild Shape.

## Fluid Shifting

At 6th level, you can change from one animal form to another as a Move Action without first returning to your normal form. This expends one use of Wild Shape and does not extend the duration of the transformation.

## Moonbeam

At 10th level, you can prepare Moonbeam as a 2nd-level Druid spell. It is normally a 3rd-level Druid spell.

## True Form

At 14th level, you can prepare True Form as a 4th-level Druid spell. It is normally a 5th-level Druid spell.

## Lunar Mastery

At 18th level, you can use Fluid Shifting once per turn without expending an additional use of Wild Shape. The new form must be one you are currently able to assume, and the transformation's original duration continues to apply.

## Archdruid

At 20th level, you can use Wild Shape as a Move Action, and your Wild Shape can last until you dismiss it or become unconscious.
