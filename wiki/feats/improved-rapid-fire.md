# Improved Rapid Fire

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 15; BAB +6; [Rapid Fire](rapid-fire.md).

When you use Rapid Fire, you no longer take its -2 penalty on attack rolls.

**Required for:** [Many Shot](many-shot.md).
