# Multi-Attack

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Three or more natural attacks, granted natively by a Shifter form profile or dual-wielding framework.

Your mastery of natural weaponry allows you to coordinate rapid, multi-limbed strikes with minimal loss of accuracy.

**Benefit:** When executing a Full Attack routine using both primary and secondary natural weapons, the penalty applied to all Secondary Natural Attacks is reduced from -5 to -2. This boosts the accuracy of off-hand weapon slots 1 and 2 by +3.

**Required for:** None.
