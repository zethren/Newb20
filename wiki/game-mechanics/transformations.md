# Transformation Rules

While a creature is transformed into another form, treat it as that form's race for racial traits. This applies to Primal Shifter forms, polymorph effects, and Druid Wild Shape.

The transformation suppresses the creature's original ancestry-based racial traits and replaces them with the transformed form's racial package. This includes racial ability bonuses, vision (such as low-light or darkvision), scent and its range, movement speeds, and biological traits such as reproductive capability. Apply the active form's package instead of combining it with the original ancestry's racial package.

The creature retains its base ability scores and nonracial bonuses unless the transformation effect states otherwise. Apply ability-score bonuses according to the [bonus type and stacking rules](../attributes.md#ability-score-bonus-types); in particular, Size bonuses and racial form bonuses are separate types.

When the transformation ends, the creature's original ancestry-based racial traits return.

## Hengeyokai Shapechanging (Draft)

Hengeyokai can assume their natural animal, hybrid, or humanoid form. Their chosen +2 ability bonus and Size persist in every form, but animal-type benefits depend on the form: humanoid form suppresses them, hybrid form grants natural attacks and partial sensory benefits, and natural animal form grants the full animal package.

These form-specific benefits do not grant general polymorph ability-score or natural-armor adjustments. See the [Hengeyokai animal-form profiles](../races.md#hengeyokai-animal-forms-draft) for the exact benefits by type and form.

A Hengeyokai humanoid form may visually resemble any listed base ancestry, but appearance grants none of that ancestry's mechanics. The Hengeyokai's Size and statistics do not change to match the chosen appearance.

The proposed action cost to change form is one standard action. The action cost and whether shifting provokes interruption remain to be finalized with the general action rules. In natural animal form, a Hengeyokai cannot speak or manipulate objects with hands. Its hybrid and humanoid forms retain speech and manual dexterity.

The proposed equipment rule is that worn and carried items meld into the Hengeyokai's body and are inaccessible in natural animal form, then return when it changes back. Confirm this against the shared item-transformation rules before finalizing.

## Item and Action Limits

### Primal Shifter Forms

In Primal Shifter forms, the character has no manual dexterity and cannot speak. They cannot drink potions or activate magic items that require verbal or somatic components. Only items with the **Shifter** enchantment property remain with the character; all other worn or carried items are dropped when the form is assumed.

### Polymorph and Wild Shape

Under ordinary Polymorph effects and Druid Wild Shape, worn and carried items meld into the transformed body and are inaccessible until the transformation ends. They are not dropped.

### Baleful Polymorph and Transformative Curses

Baleful Polymorph and curses that physically change a creature into another form use the Primal Shifter item rule: the transformed creature cannot speak or use manual dexterity, and drops all worn or carried items except those with the **Shifter** enchantment property.
