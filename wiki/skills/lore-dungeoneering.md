# Lore (Dungeoneering)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Dungeoneering) covers knowledge of underground environments, subterranean hazards, and creatures and structures associated with them.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Dungeoneering) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Survival](survival.md) checks to navigate or endure underground environments.
