# Races

## Base Ancestries

The current base ancestry roster is:

- Human
- Elf
- Dwarf
- Half-Orc
- Halfling
- Half-Elf
- Gnome

## Planar-Blooded Heritages

Aasimar and Tiefling are planar-blooded heritages that can be combined with any base ancestry listed above. Each combined ancestry uses one **10-RP budget**. The base ancestry's retained ability adjustments and Size package count toward that budget.

Energy Resistance 5 protects against matching energy damage and allows comfortable existence in environments of that energy type without environmental hazard damage or associated maluses.

### Tiefling (Draft)

- A Tiefling retains the base ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Tiefling heritage replaces that ancestry's other special traits.
- **Infernal:** Energy Resistance 5 against fire (3 RP, provisional).
- **Abyssal:** Energy Resistance 5 against cold (3 RP, provisional). Acid resistance is not included.
- **Shared Tiefling Trait — Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP, provisional).
- **Shared Tiefling Trait — Prehensile Tail:** Tieflings have a prehensile tail. The [Tiefling Tail Feats](feats/tiefling-tail-feats.md) let a character use it to handle items and, with further training, wield a weapon or shield.

The chosen resistance and Darkvision provisionally cost **5 RP**. The tail's presence has no separate RP cost; its functions are feat-gated. The basic and advanced feats are ordinary feat selections, not ancestry-granted features, so they do not consume the ancestry's RP unless a future rule grants one for free.

### Aasimar (Draft)

- An Aasimar retains the base ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Aasimar heritage replaces that ancestry's other special traits.
- **Resist Electricity:** Energy Resistance 5 against electricity (3 RP, provisional).
- **Resist Acid:** Energy Resistance 5 against acid (3 RP, provisional).
- **Shared Aasimar Trait — Wings:** Aasimars have wings. Flight and controlled descent require the [Aasimar Wing Feats](feats/aasimar-wing-feats.md).

Choose one resistance. The resistance type is the only difference between the Electricity and Acid variants. The wings' presence has no separate RP cost, and their flight benefits require ordinary feat selections; those feats are not granted automatically and do not consume the ancestry's RP.

## Hengeyokai (Draft)

Hengeyokai are spirit creatures with material bodies and animal identities. They are born either to a Hengeyokai mother of the same animal type or when a spirit inhabits an animal and awakens it to higher sentience. A Hengeyokai can change among its natural animal, hybrid, and humanoid forms.

### Shared Hengeyokai Rules

- **Three Forms:** A Hengeyokai can assume its natural animal form, a hybrid form, or a humanoid form. The proposed shifting action is one standard action; the final action cost remains to be confirmed.
- **Ability Bonus:** The +2 Racial bonus to one ability score chosen at character creation applies in all three forms. No other form grants another ancestry's racial traits or class-form statistics.
- **Animal Anatomy:** In natural animal form, the Hengeyokai cannot speak or manipulate objects with hands. The hybrid and humanoid forms can speak and use their hands.
- **Equipment:** Proposed rule: equipment melds and becomes inaccessible in animal form, then returns when the Hengeyokai leaves that form. Confirm this against the shared transformation and item rules.

| Form tier | Benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Humanoid | The chosen +2 ability bonus only. Animal-type traits, movement, senses, and natural attacks are suppressed. Appearance grants no other ancestry's mechanics. |
| Hybrid | The chosen +2 ability bonus, natural attacks, and the partial sensory benefits listed below. No animal-form movement or non-sensory animal traits such as skill bonuses or natural armor. |
| Natural animal | The chosen +2 ability bonus and the full animal-type package, including movement, senses, skill traits, natural armor if listed, and natural attacks. No general Beast Shape ability-score or natural-armor bonuses. |

### Shared Race-Point Budget

Each Hengeyokai type uses one 10-RP budget:

| +2 Racial bonus to one ability score chosen at character creation | 3 RP |

| **Target total** | **10 RP; not yet balanced** |

The earlier 4-RP shifting and 3-RP animal-package prices assumed fewer form benefits and are not validated for these rules. Reassess the shared and type packages before treating the 10-RP target as balanced.

### Kitsune (Separate Spirit Classification)

Kitsune are fox spirits with a stronger connection to the spirit realm and a special capacity for magic. They are a distinct classification rather than a Fox Hengeyokai type, and do not qualify for the Hengeyokai Shifter Talent feat. They use the shared three-form rules and fox animal-form profile, but their exact spirit-magic package, spell options, and ancestry cost remain open; Kitsune are not yet a complete 10-RP draft. No Fox Shifter form is defined.

Kitsune begin with one tail. Additional tails are earned by selecting Kitsune tail feats; each feat grants a distinct tail gift. These feats are optional and are not included in the Kitsune's ancestry RP budget. See [Kitsune Tail Feats](feats/kitsune-tail-feats.md).

The proposed tail gifts are +1 to one ability score, +2 to one skill, or limited spell recall. Ability scores and skills cannot be selected more than once across a character's tails. Spell recall's level limit, frequency, and interaction with spell slots or Sorcery Points remain provisional.

## Ancestry Point Budget (Draft)

Each final ancestry package, including a base ancestry combined with a planar heritage, uses **10 Race Points (RP)**. Retained ability adjustments and Size packages count toward that same budget. Drawbacks may refund points. The costs below establish provisional benchmarks; non-numeric traits and drawback values still require comparative review.

| Trait benchmark | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one fixed ability score | 2 RP |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice | 3 RP |
| One additional feat at character creation | 4 RP |
| +1 skill rank per character level | 2 RP (provisional) |
| +2 Racial bonus to one named skill | 1 RP |
| One additional skill designated as a class skill | 1 RP (provisional) |
| Weapon familiarity with up to 3 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 1 RP (provisional) |
| Weapon familiarity with 6 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 2 RP (provisional) |
| Minor racial ability | 1 RP |
| Major racial ability | 3 RP |
| Exceptional or feat-equivalent ability | 4 RP |
| Small Size package, including all listed Size effects | 3 RP net |

Classify non-numeric abilities by their breadth, frequency, level scaling, and combat impact.

Ability adjustments use **+2 increments**. Larger adjustments cost the listed amount for each +2 increment.

Mechanically meaningful drawbacks may refund points, but the combined refund is capped at **2 RP** per ancestry package. Purely narrative limitations do not refund points.

## Human (Draft)

- **Flexible Ability (3 RP):** +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice.
- **Bonus Feat (4 RP):** One additional feat at character creation; the character must meet its prerequisites.
- **Skilled (2 RP, provisional):** Gain 1 additional skill rank to spend per character level, subject to the normal maximum ranks per skill.

The package currently totals **9 RP** at the revised provisional price. Allocate the remaining 1 RP after the Human skill customization is finalized.

## Elf (Draft)

- **Low-Light Vision:** See in dim light better than humans do (1 RP, provisional).
- **Keen Senses:** +2 Racial bonus to Perception (1 RP, provisional).
- **Magical Sleep Immunity:** Immune to magical effects that cause sleep (1 RP, provisional).
- **Four-Hour Long Rest:** Complete a Long Rest in 4 hours instead of 8 (1 RP, provisional), as a real-time quality-of-life benefit.
- **Stealth:** +2 Racial bonus to Stealth checks (1 RP).
- **Lore (Nature):** +2 Racial bonus to Lore (Nature) checks (1 RP).
- **Weapon Familiarity (2 RP, provisional):** Elves are proficient with longswords, rapiers, longbows (including composite longbows), shortbows (including composite shortbows), and weapons with "Elven" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.

The Elf chooses one ability adjustment:

- **Dexterity:** +2 Racial bonus to Dexterity (2 RP).
- **Charisma subrace:** +2 Racial bonus to Charisma (2 RP), replacing the Dexterity adjustment.
- **Wisdom subrace:** +2 Racial bonus to Wisdom (2 RP), replacing the Dexterity adjustment.

The two subrace names are not yet defined. The common 30-ft land speed costs no RP. The listed non-ability traits cost 8 RP at their provisional values; adding any one of the three +2 ability adjustments brings the package to **10 RP**. The Four-Hour Long Rest is priced as a real-time quality-of-life benefit; reassess if halving in-game time materially affects encounters or quest timing. Enchantment Defense has been removed from the current draft. A previously proposed -2 Constitution adjustment is undecided and is not included in this total.

## Dwarf (Draft)

- **Resilient:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution (2 RP).
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP, provisional).
- **Poison Immunity:** Immune to poison (4 RP, provisional), replacing the 1-RP alternative of a +2 Racial bonus on saving throws against poison.
- **Dwarven Movement (1 RP, provisional):** Base land speed is 25 ft. Armor and encumbrance do not reduce this speed, but a Heavily Encumbered Dwarf still cannot move.
- **Weapon Familiarity (1 RP, provisional):** Dwarves are proficient with battleaxes, heavy picks, warhammers, and weapons with "Dwarven" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.

The movement cost treats the 25-ft speed and ignoring armor or encumbrance speed reductions as one combined trait. Poison Immunity is provisionally priced as an exceptional ability because it negates poison rather than reducing its effects. Together with the narrower weapon list, the package totals **10 RP**. Choosing the 1-RP poison-resistance alternative instead leaves 3 RP unspent.

## Half-Orc (Draft)

- **Powerful:** +2 Racial bonus to Strength (2 RP).
- **Hardy:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution (2 RP).
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP, provisional).
- **Poor Scent (3 RP, provisional):** Scent range is 15 ft for both passive detection and active Sniff tracking. The short range limits its reach, but it still provides passive protection against unseen attackers and active tracking. See [Scent and Tracking](game-mechanics/scent.md).
- **Weapon Familiarity (1 RP, provisional):** Half-Orcs are proficient with greataxes, falchions, and weapons with "Orc" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.

The package totals **10 RP** at the current provisional costs. Intimidating has been removed to account for Scent's revised price. The Intelligence penalty is not part of this draft.

## Halfling (Draft)

- **Small Size package (3 RP net):** The Small row of the [size modifiers table](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-modifiers) grants +1 Attack and AC, -1 MAB and MAD, **+2 Dexterity and -2 Strength Size adjustments**, +4 to all Stealth checks, 5-ft reach, and 20-ft base land speed. Small-sized weapons deal less damage according to the [generic weapon damage table](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size). Small creatures also use half rations, count themselves and their carried items as one-third normal weight, can ride Medium-or-larger mounts, fit into spaces too small for Medium creatures, and share a 5-ft square without a crowding penalty as described in [Combat Scaling](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#sharing-a-5-ft-square). The 3-RP net price includes all of these Size benefits and drawbacks; do not price the listed effects separately.
- **Low-Light Vision:** See in dim light better than humans do (1 RP, provisional).
- **Stealth:** +2 Racial bonus to Stealth checks (1 RP, provisional).
- **Halfling Luck (5 RP, provisional):** +1 Racial bonus on all saving throws and all skill checks.

The named racial traits total **7 RP** at provisional costs. With the Small Size package, the Halfling totals **10 RP**. Fearless has been removed to account for Halfling Luck's revised price. Racial bonuses from these traits do not stack with one another; use the highest bonus to a given check under the [bonus stacking rules](attributes.md#bonus-types-and-stacking). Thus the racial Stealth bonus remains +2; fear saves receive the +1 bonus from Halfling Luck. The distinct +4 Size bonus to Stealth stacks with the +2 Racial bonus.

## Provisional Cross-Ancestry Balance Review

Most base ancestry drafts currently total 10 RP under the listed provisional prices; Human totals 9 RP with 1 RP intentionally unallocated. Equal totals are not evidence of equal power; these are the main comparisons to revisit as the rest of the rules are finalized:

- **Human:** The bonus feat and per-level skill rank scale differently with level and depend on the size and quality of the feat and skill options available.
- **Elf:** The 4-hour Long Rest is priced as a minor real-time convenience; revisit its value if halving in-game time materially affects encounters or quest timing. Its weapon familiarity covers a broader list than the Dwarf and Half-Orc versions.
- **Dwarf:** Immunity to all poison is provisionally priced at 4 RP as a complete negation, but its value still depends on how common and severe poisons are. Dwarven Movement combines a lower base speed with ignoring armor and encumbrance speed reductions.
- **Half-Orc:** Scent is provisionally priced at 3 RP because it grants both passive defense against unseen attackers and active tracking; its value still depends on how often scent can reveal hidden creatures and bypass concealment.
- **Halfling:** Small Size has a wide range of benefits and drawbacks, while Halfling Luck is provisionally priced at 5 RP for applying to every saving throw and skill check. Both packages need review against actual encounter and skill use.
- **Half-Elf:** The flexible ability bonus, extra class skill, broad weapon familiarity, and overlapping Perception/enchantment defenses should be compared with the narrower traits in other ancestries.
- **Gnome:** Illusion Affinity and Illusion Resistance cannot be fully valued until the spell schools, spell DCs, and Illusion effects are defined.
- **Weapon familiarity:** The current 1/2-RP split is based on list breadth, not measured combat value. Revisit it when the weapon catalog and proficiency rules are complete.
