# Apex Predator

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +1.

You leverage your superior physical mass to overwhelm smaller threats, pinning them down and breaking through their desperate attempts to evade you.

**Benefit:** Against a target at least one size category smaller than you, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all Attack Rolls (AB) and Martial Ability Bonus (MAB) checks. For example, a Huge bear attacking a Medium human or a Large wolf attacking a Small halfling qualifies.

**Required for:** None.
