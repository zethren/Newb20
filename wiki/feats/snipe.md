# Snipe

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Point Blank Shot](point-blank-shot.md); BAB +4.

**Benefit:** Your ranged weapon attacks ignore the AC bonus granted by cover. You cannot attack a target through total cover, and this feat does not bypass concealment or other miss chances.

**Required for:** None.
