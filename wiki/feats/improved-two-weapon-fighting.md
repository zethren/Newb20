# Improved Two-Weapon Fighting

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 17; BAB +6; [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md).

When you make a Full Attack Action while wielding a weapon in each hand, the penalty on the off-hand attack granted by Two-Weapon Fighting is reduced from -2 to 0. This does not grant an additional off-hand attack.

**Required for:** [Greater Two-Weapon Fighting](greater-two-weapon-fighting.md).
