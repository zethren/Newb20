# Lore (Nobility)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Nobility) covers noble houses, heraldry, titles, court customs, and political hierarchies.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Nobility) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Persuasion](persuasion.md) checks when interacting with courts or nobility.
