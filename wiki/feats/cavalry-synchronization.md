# Cavalry Synchronization

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md); BAB +3.

You possess a flawless baseline muscle memory that lets you effortlessly absorb the shock and recoil of your steed's offensive movements.

**Benefit:** When your mount executes an attack roll or a full cascading iterative routine, the scaling Ride DC required to stay seated in the saddle is permanently reduced by your BAB + Dexterity modifier.

**Required for:** None.
