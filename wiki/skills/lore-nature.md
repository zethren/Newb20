# Lore (Nature)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Nature) covers animals, plants, weather, natural environments, and ecological patterns. It includes the former Knowledge (Nature).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Nature) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Survival](survival.md) checks in natural environments. This Synergy bonus stacks with Nature Sense's Supernatural bonus.
