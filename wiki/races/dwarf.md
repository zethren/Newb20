# Dwarf

- **Resilient:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution.
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft.
- **Poison Immunity:** Immune to poison.
- **Dwarven Movement:** Base land speed is 25 ft. Armor and encumbrance do not reduce this speed, but a Heavily Encumbered Dwarf still cannot move.
- **Weapon Familiarity:** Proficient with battleaxes, heavy picks, warhammers, and weapons with "Dwarven" in their name. This proficiency prevents the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.
