# Weapon Training

**Class:** Fighter 3, 6, 11, 16

The Fighter's weapon proficiency list sorts into the Weapon Training groups below.

| Weapon Training group | Weapons |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy Blades and Axes | Greatsword, greataxe |
| Blunt and Hafted Weapons | Maul, club, mace, morningstar, quarterstaff |
| Swords and Knives | Longsword, shortsword, dagger, throwing knives |
| Polearms | Spear, halberd, bardiche |
| Bows | Longbow, shortbow |
| Crossbows and Slings | Crossbow, heavy crossbow, sling |

At 3rd, 6th, 11th, and 16th level, the Fighter chooses a Weapon Training group and gains a **+1 Supernatural bonus to attack and damage rolls** with weapons in that group. The Fighter may choose the same group multiple times, stacking the bonuses, or choose a different group to train with it. The Fighter may train in up to four different groups by 16th level.

A Weapon Training group is a mechanical attack-and-damage grouping. It is distinct from an ancestry's weapon-familiarity group (see [Races](../../../../races.md)), and the two do not interact.
