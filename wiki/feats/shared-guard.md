# Shared Guard

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisites:** None.

While you and a conscious ally who also has this feat are both within 5 feet of the same enemy, you and that ally each gain a +1 Teamwork bonus to Dodge AC against that enemy's attacks.

**Required for:** None.
