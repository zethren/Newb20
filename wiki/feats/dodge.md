# Dodge

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13.

**Benefit:** At the start of your turn, choose one creature you can see as your primary target. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 Circumstance bonus to Dodge AC against attacks made by that creature. You can have only one primary target at a time.

**Required for:** [Mobility](mobility.md); [Spring Attack](spring-attack.md).
