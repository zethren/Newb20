# Sense Motive

**Key ability:** Wisdom

Sense Motive uses observation and judgment to assess another creature's intentions, sincerity, or emotional state.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Sense Motive grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Persuasion](persuasion.md) checks made after assessing the other participant's intentions.
