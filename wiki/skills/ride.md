# Ride

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Ride covers controlling a mount, remaining mounted in difficult conditions, and directing a mount during travel or combat.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Ride grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Handle Animal](handle-animal.md) checks involving a mount the character regularly rides.
