# Hit and Run

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raven form), Tier 6

**Action:** Selectable, taken after a standard action attack.

After making a standard action attack, the Raven can take movement actions this turn. This is an exception to the rule that a standard action attack ends movement; see [Movement After a Standard Action Attack](../../../../game-mechanics.md#movement-after-a-standard-action-attack).

* **No Provocation:** Movement from Hit and Run does not provoke Attacks of Opportunity from the creature the Raven attacked.
* **Movement Cap:** The Raven's total movement in a round, including Hit and Run, cannot exceed its maximum movement. For the Raven, this is its flight speed (60 ft at Tier 6).
