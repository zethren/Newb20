# Improved Vanguard Intercept

**Class:** Bodyguard 6

The Bodyguard improves their [Vanguard Intercept](../../../../feats/vanguard-intercept.md) when protecting either Protected Charge.

* **Range:** Either Protected Charge can be up to 10 feet away when targeted by a physical melee or ranged attack.
* **Check Bonus:** The Bodyguard gains a **+2 bonus to MAB** on the Vanguard Intercept check.
* **On a Success:** The Bodyguard moves into the Charge's square. The Charge moves to the next available square behind the Bodyguard, relative to the attacker, and the attack is redirected to the Bodyguard.
* **On a Failure:** The attack is not redirected and resolves against its original target. The Bodyguard moves to an available square adjacent to the Charge.
