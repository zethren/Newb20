# Warden's Shield

**Class:** Bodyguard 1

When the Bodyguard uses [Raise Shield Other](../../../../feats/defender-combat-feats.md) to protect one of their Protected Charges, that charge gains the shield's AC bonus and the Bodyguard does not lose that bonus to their own defense. This still protects only the single ally chosen for that use of Raise Shield Other.
