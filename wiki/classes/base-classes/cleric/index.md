# Cleric

Clerics are divine spellcasters who serve a deity, sacred cause, or spiritual tradition. They combine prepared divine magic with martial training and choose two alignment-appropriate domains at character creation.

## Class Features

### Spellcasting

The Cleric prepares divine spells and uses Wisdom for spellcasting. A Cleric's deity or tradition determines which spells and domains are available. The Cleric uses the shared full-caster spell-level progression in [Classes](../../index.md#full-spell-level-progression).

See the [Cleric spell list](../../../spell-lists/cleric.md) for available spells and individual spell rules.

### Channel Divinity

**Class:** Cleric 1

As a Standard Action, expend one use of Channel Divinity to release a wave of divine energy. You can use Channel Divinity a number of times per Long Rest equal to your Cleric level + your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 use). Creatures of your choice within 30 feet are affected according to their creature type. The effect is **1d6 per two Cleric levels, rounded up** (maximum 10d6). When the effect heals a creature, add your Charisma modifier once to the result (minimum 0 HP restored).

The damage type is positive for a Cleric whose alignment is Good, and negative energy for a Cleric whose alignment is Evil. If your alignment is Neutral on the good-evil axis, choose positive or negative energy each time you use Channel Divinity. Positive energy heals living creatures and damages undead; negative energy damages living creatures and heals undead. Creatures damaged by Channel Divinity can attempt a Reflex save (DC = 10 + half your Cleric level, rounded down, + your Wisdom modifier) to take half damage. A save does not reduce healing.

## Divine Domains

Choose two different domains at level 1. You must meet each domain's alignment requirement. Each domain grants one power at level 1 and another at level 8. Each activated domain power can be used once per Short Rest, with its uses tracked independently. See [Cleric Domains](domains.md) for the domain categories, eligibility, and powers.
