# Cleric Domains

A Cleric chooses **two different domains** at level 1. Each domain grants its first power at Cleric level 1 and its second power at Cleric level 8. The Cleric must meet the alignment requirement for each chosen domain. A Cleric can select two domains from the same category if eligible.

Unless a power states otherwise, each activated domain power can be used once per Short Rest; each power has its own uses and they do not share a pool. Passive powers do not require activation or expend a use. A domain power save DC is **10 + half your Cleric level, rounded down, + your Wisdom modifier**.

## Alignment Eligibility

Alignment categories refer to either axis of a character's alignment:

| Category | Eligible alignments |
| --- | --- |
| Lawful | Lawful Good, Lawful Neutral, Lawful Evil |
| Good | Lawful Good, Neutral Good, Chaotic Good |
| Neutral | Any alignment with a Neutral component: Lawful Neutral, Neutral Good, True Neutral, Chaotic Neutral, or Neutral Evil |
| Chaotic | Chaotic Good, Chaotic Neutral, Chaotic Evil |
| Evil | Lawful Evil, Neutral Evil, Chaotic Evil |

## Lawful Domains

### Order

- **Command (Level 1):** As a Standard Action, choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear and understand you. It must succeed at a Will save or follow one brief command you give it on its next turn, such as approach, flee, drop, or kneel. It will not directly harm itself.
- **Stalwart Edict (Level 8):** As a Reaction when an ally within 30 feet fails a saving throw, let that ally reroll it. The ally must use the new result.

### Protection

- **Protective Ward (Level 1):** As a Standard Action, grant one creature within 30 feet a **+2 Supernatural bonus to Shield AC** until the start of your next turn.
- **Guardian's Rebuke (Level 8):** As a Reaction when an adjacent ally takes damage, reduce that damage by **1d8 + your Wisdom modifier** (minimum 1).

### War

- **Battle Focus (Level 1):** As a Free Action, gain a **+1 Supernatural bonus on weapon attack rolls** for 1 minute.
- **Rallying Strike (Level 8):** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, choose one ally within 30 feet. The next weapon attack that ally hits before the start of your next turn deals an extra **1d6 damage**.

## Good Domains

### Life

- **Empowered Healing (Level 1):** Once per Short Rest, when a spell or feature you use restores HP to one or more creatures, choose one of those creatures to regain additional HP equal to **1d6 + your Wisdom modifier** (minimum 1).
- **Preserve Life (Level 8):** As a Standard Action, choose up to three creatures within 30 feet. Each regains HP equal to **2d8 + your Wisdom modifier**.

### Light

- **Radiant Flare (Level 1):** As a Standard Action, choose one creature within 60 feet. It must succeed at a Reflex save or take **1d8 holy damage**. Holy damage harms living and undead creatures and never restores HP.
- **Revealing Radiance (Level 8):** As a Standard Action, shed revealing light in a 30-foot radius for 1 minute. Invisible creatures in the area are visible while they remain in it; this light does not dispel invisibility.

## Neutral Domains

### Knowledge

- **Lorekeeper (Level 1):** When you make an Intelligence-based Lore or Investigation check, you can add a **+2 Supernatural bonus** to the check.
- **Read the Pattern (Level 8):** As a Standard Action, study one creature within 30 feet. Until the start of your next turn, you and your allies gain a **+2 Supernatural bonus on attack rolls** against it.

### Nature

- **Wild Communion (Level 1):** You can communicate simple ideas with ordinary animals. This passive ability does not compel them to help you and does not expend a use.
- **Animal Companion (Level 8):** You gain an animal companion with an effective companion level equal to half your Cleric level, rounded down (minimum 1). See [Animal Companions](../../../game-mechanics/animal-companions.md) for initiative and command rules.

## Chaotic Domains

### Trickery

- **Misdirection (Level 1):** As a Reaction when a creature makes an attack roll against you, impose a **-2 Circumstance penalty** on that roll.
- **False Double (Level 8):** As a Reaction when an attack hits you, impose a **50% miss chance** on that attack. If it misses, the attack deals no damage to you.

### Liberation

- **Unbound Step (Level 1):** As a Move Action, move up to your speed. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **Break Bonds (Level 8):** As a Free Action, end one effect that is Grappling, Pinned, or Restrained you, provided the effect allows escape through physical movement or a check.

## Evil Domains

### Death

- **Grave Touch (Level 1):** As a Standard Action, make a melee touch attack against a creature. On a hit, a living creature takes **1d8 negative energy damage**; an undead creature instead regains **1d8 HP**.
- **Death's Claim (Level 8):** As a Standard Action, choose one creature within 30 feet that has half or fewer of its maximum HP. It must succeed at a Fortitude save or become Staggered until the end of its next turn.

### Tyranny

- **Dread Command (Level 1):** As a Standard Action, choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed at a Will save or become [Shaken](../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#shaken) until the start of your next turn.
- **Crushing Authority (Level 8):** As a Standard Action, choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed at a Will save or be unable to take Reactions and take a **-2 Circumstance penalty on attack rolls** until the start of your next turn.

Channel Divinity's energy type follows the Cleric's alignment on the good-evil axis; see [Cleric](index.md#channel-divinity).
