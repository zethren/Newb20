# Alpha's Howl

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Wolf form), Tier 5

**Action:** Free Action; once per encounter.

The Wolf howls across a 60-foot radius. Each ally in the area can immediately take a Reaction to move up to its base land speed or make one melee weapon attack. Each enemy in the area must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half the Shifter level + the Shifter's Wisdom modifier) or become Shaken for 1d4 rounds.
