# Kitsune

Kitsune are fox spirits with a strong connection to the spirit realm and a special capacity for magic. They are a distinct spirit ancestry and do not qualify for the [Hengeyokai Shifter Talent](../feats/hengeyokai-shifter-talent.md).

Kitsune can assume humanoid, hybrid, and fox forms. The rules below apply to these Kitsune forms.

## Fox Form

Fox form has a 40-ft. land speed and Scent with a 15-ft. range. Hybrid form has Scent with a 5-ft. range.

In fox and hybrid forms, a Kitsune has a bite (main hand, 1d4 Medium base damage) and two claws (off hand, 1d3 Medium base damage each). The bite deals blunt, piercing, and slashing damage; the claws deal piercing and slashing damage. Apply the shared [natural-weapon rules](../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#natural-weapon-properties) and [weapon damage by size](../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size).

## Magical Tails

Kitsune gain additional magical tails as they advance. Each tail grants a chosen Ioun Stone effect, as described in [Kitsune Tail Feats](../feats/kitsune-tail-feats.md).
