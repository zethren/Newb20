# Feats

## Animal and Natural-Weapon Feats

[Animal Feats](feats/animal-feats.md) contains the natural-weapon and predator options, including Multi-Attack, Swallow Whole, Rend, Trample, and Leaping Pounce.

## Defender Feats

[Defender Combat Feats](feats/defender-combat-feats.md) contains Stand Ground, Vanguard Intercept, and Raise Shield Other.

## Martial Maneuver Feats

[Martial Maneuver Feats](feats/maneuver-feats.md) contains Improved Trip and other maneuver-specific options.

## Ancestry Feats

- [Tiefling Tail Feats](feats/tiefling-tail-feats.md): Prehensile Tail and Tail Combat.
- [Aasimar Wing Feats](feats/aasimar-wing-feats.md): Featherfall Wings and Poor Flight.
- [Kitsune Tail Feats](feats/kitsune-tail-feats.md): optional additional-tail progression and spirit gifts.
- [Hengeyokai Shifter Talent](feats/hengeyokai-shifter-talent.md): an immediate bonus Shifter Talent selection for the matching animal form.

## Mounted Feats and Rules

[Mounted Combat](game-mechanics/mounted-combat.md) documents mount rules alongside Mounted Combat, Cavalry Synchronization, Savage Cavalry, and mounted defensive options.
