## Planar-Blooded Heritages

Aasimar and Tiefling are planar-blooded heritages that can be combined with any base ancestry listed above. Each combined ancestry uses one **10-RP budget**. The base ancestry's retained ability adjustments and Size package count toward that budget.

Energy Resistance 5 protects against matching energy damage and allows comfortable existence in environments of that energy type without environmental hazard damage or associated maluses.

### Tiefling

- A Tiefling retains the base ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Tiefling heritage replaces that ancestry's other special traits.
- **Infernal:** Energy Resistance 5 against fire (3 RP).
- **Abyssal:** Energy Resistance 5 against cold (3 RP). Acid resistance is not included.
- **Shared Tiefling Trait — Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP).
- **Shared Tiefling Trait — Prehensile Tail:** Tieflings have a prehensile tail. The [Tiefling Tail Feats](feats/tiefling-tail-feats.md) let a character use it to handle items and, with further training, wield a weapon or shield.

The chosen resistance and Darkvision cost **5 RP**. The tail's presence has no separate RP cost; its functions are feat-gated. The basic and advanced feats are ordinary feat selections, not ancestry-granted features, so they do not consume the ancestry's RP.

### Aasimar

- An Aasimar retains the base ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Aasimar heritage replaces that ancestry's other special traits.
- **Resist Electric:** Energy Resistance 5 against electric (3 RP).
- **Resist Acid:** Energy Resistance 5 against acid (3 RP).
- **Shared Aasimar Trait — Wings:** Aasimars have wings. Flight and controlled descent require the [Aasimar Wing Feats](feats/aasimar-wing-feats.md).

Choose one resistance. The resistance type is the only difference between the Electric and Acid variants. The wings' presence has no separate RP cost, and their flight benefits require ordinary feat selections; those feats are not granted automatically and do not consume the ancestry's RP.

## Hengeyokai

Hengeyokai are spirit creatures with material bodies and animal identities. They are born either to a Hengeyokai mother of the same animal type or when a spirit inhabits an animal and awakens it to higher sentience. A Hengeyokai can change among its natural animal, hybrid, and humanoid forms.

### Shared Hengeyokai Rules

- **Three Forms:** A Hengeyokai can assume its natural animal form, a hybrid form, or a humanoid form. The proposed shifting action is one standard action; the final action cost remains to be confirmed.
- **Ability Bonus:** The +2 Racial bonus to one ability score chosen at character creation applies in all three forms. No other form grants another ancestry's racial traits or class-form statistics.
- **Animal Anatomy:** In natural animal form, the Hengeyokai cannot speak or manipulate objects with hands. The hybrid and humanoid forms can speak and use their hands.
- **Equipment:** Hengeyokai follow the [Natural Shifter](equipment.md#natural-shifter) transformation rules for equipment.

| Form tier | Benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Humanoid | The chosen +2 ability bonus only. Animal-type traits, movement, senses, and natural attacks are suppressed. Appearance grants no other ancestry's mechanics. |
| Hybrid | The chosen +2 ability bonus, natural attacks, and the partial sensory benefits listed below. No animal-form movement or non-sensory animal traits such as skill bonuses or natural armor. |
| Natural animal | The chosen +2 ability bonus and the full animal-type package, including movement, senses, skill traits, natural armor if listed, and natural attacks. No general Beast Shape ability-score or natural-armor bonuses. |

### Shared Race-Point Budget

Each Hengeyokai type uses one 10-RP budget. The budget includes the shared traits and one animal-type package.

## Human

- **Flexible Ability (3 RP):** +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice.
- **Bonus Feat (4 RP):** One additional feat at character creation; the character must meet its prerequisites.
- **Skilled (2 RP):** Gain 1 additional skill rank to spend per character level, subject to the normal maximum ranks per skill.



## Elf

- **Low-Light Vision:** See in dim light better than humans do (1 RP).
- **Keen Senses:** +2 Racial bonus to Perception (1 RP).
- **Magical Sleep Immunity:** Immune to magical effects that cause sleep (1 RP).
- **Four-Hour Long Rest:** Complete a Long Rest in 4 hours instead of 8 (1 RP), as a real-time quality-of-life benefit.
- **Stealth:** +2 Racial bonus to Stealth checks (1 RP).
- **Lore (Nature):** +2 Racial bonus to Lore (Nature) checks (1 RP).
- **Weapon Familiarity (2 RP):** Elves are proficient with longswords, rapiers, longbows (including composite longbows), shortbows (including composite shortbows), and weapons with "Elven" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.

The Elf chooses one ability adjustment:

- **Dexterity:** +2 Racial bonus to Dexterity (2 RP).
- **Charisma subrace:** +2 Racial bonus to Charisma (2 RP), replacing the Dexterity adjustment.
- **Wisdom subrace:** +2 Racial bonus to Wisdom (2 RP), replacing the Dexterity adjustment.



## Dwarf

- **Resilient:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution (2 RP).
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP).
- **Poison Immunity:** Immune to poison (4 RP), replacing the 1-RP alternative of a +2 Racial bonus on saving throws against poison.
- **Dwarven Movement (1 RP):** Base land speed is 25 ft. Armor and encumbrance do not reduce this speed, but a Heavily Encumbered Dwarf still cannot move.
- **Weapon Familiarity (1 RP):** Dwarves are proficient with battleaxes, heavy picks, warhammers, and weapons with "Dwarven" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.



## Half-Orc

- **Powerful:** +2 Racial bonus to Strength (2 RP).
- **Hardy:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution (2 RP).
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP).
- **Poor Scent (3 RP):** Scent range is 15 ft for both passive detection and active Sniff tracking. The short range limits its reach, but it still provides passive protection against unseen attackers and active tracking. See [Scent and Tracking](game-mechanics/scent.md).
- **Weapon Familiarity (1 RP):** Half-Orcs are proficient with greataxes, falchions, and weapons with "Orc" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.



## Halfling

- **Small Size package (3 RP net):** The Small row of the [size modifiers table](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-modifiers) grants +1 Attack and AC, -1 MAB and MAD, **+2 Dexterity and -2 Strength Size adjustments**, +4 to all Stealth checks, 5-ft reach, and 20-ft base land speed. Small-sized weapons deal less damage according to the [generic weapon damage table](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size). Small creatures also use half rations, count themselves and their carried items as one-third normal weight, can ride Medium-or-larger mounts, fit into spaces too small for Medium creatures, and share a 5-ft square without a crowding penalty as described in [Combat Scaling](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#sharing-a-5-ft-square). The 3-RP net price includes all of these Size benefits and drawbacks; do not price the listed effects separately.
- **Low-Light Vision:** See in dim light better than humans do (1 RP).
- **Stealth:** +2 Racial bonus to Stealth checks (1 RP).
- **Halfling Luck (5 RP):** +1 Racial bonus on all saving throws and all skill checks.

Racial bonuses from these traits do not stack with one another; use the highest bonus to a given check under the [bonus stacking rules](attributes.md#bonus-types-and-stacking). Thus the racial Stealth bonus remains +2; fear saves receive the +1 bonus from Halfling Luck. The distinct +4 Size bonus to Stealth stacks with the +2 Racial bonus.

## Half-Elf

- **Flexible Ability (3 RP):** +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice.
- **Low-Light Vision (1 RP):** See in dim light better than humans do.
- **Adaptability (1 RP):** Designate one additional skill as a class skill for the character. The normal +3 Class Skill bonus applies.
- **Keen Senses (1 RP):** +2 Racial bonus to Perception checks.
- **Enchantment Resistance (2 RP):** +2 Racial bonus on saving throws against Enchantment effects, including magical sleep.
- **Weapon Familiarity (2 RP):** Half-Elves are proficient with longswords, rapiers, longbows (including composite longbows), shortbows (including composite shortbows), and weapons with "Elven" in their name. Proficiency with these weapons means the wielder does not take the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.

The standard Medium base land speed is 30 ft and costs no RP. These traits total **10 RP** at the current costs.

## Gnome

- **Small Size package (3 RP net):** Apply the Small row of the [size modifiers table](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-modifiers) and the [size-dependent utility rules](game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-dependent-utility), including the Size adjustments of +2 Dexterity and -2 Strength and penalty-free 5-ft square sharing.
- **Intelligent:** +2 Racial bonus to Intelligence (2 RP).
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft (2 RP).
- **Illusion Affinity (2 RP):** +1 Circumstance bonus to the spell DC of Illusion spells the Gnome casts.
- **Illusion Resistance (1 RP):** +2 Racial bonus on saving throws against Illusion effects.

These traits total **10 RP** at the current costs.

### Kitsune (Separate Spirit Classification)

Kitsune are fox spirits with a stronger connection to the spirit realm and a special capacity for magic. They are a distinct classification rather than a Fox Hengeyokai type, and do not qualify for the Hengeyokai Shifter Talent feat. They use their own three-form racial rules; their fox form is not a Primal Shifter form or tier.

Kitsune gain magical tails at 1st, 5th, 7th, 9th, 11th, 13th, 15th, 17th, and 20th level. Each tail grants one chosen effect from the [Ioun Stone effects](game-mechanics/magic-item-enchantments.md#ioun-stones). See [Kitsune Magical Tails](feats/kitsune-tail-feats.md) for details.

## Ancestry Point Budget

Each ancestry package, including a base ancestry combined with a planar heritage, uses **10 Race Points (RP)**. Retained ability adjustments and Size packages count toward that same budget. Drawbacks may refund points. Use the listed cost for each ancestry trait.

| Trait benchmark | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one fixed ability score | 2 RP |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice | 3 RP |
| One additional feat at character creation | 4 RP |
| +1 skill rank per character level | 2 RP |
| +2 Racial bonus to one named skill | 1 RP |
| One additional skill designated as a class skill | 1 RP |
| Weapon familiarity with up to 3 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 1 RP |
| Weapon familiarity with 6 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 2 RP |
| Minor racial ability | 1 RP |
| Major racial ability | 3 RP |
| Exceptional or feat-equivalent ability | 4 RP |
| Small Size package, including all listed Size effects | 3 RP net |

Classify non-numeric abilities by their breadth, frequency, level scaling, and combat impact.

Ability adjustments use **+2 increments**. Larger adjustments cost the listed amount for each +2 increment.

Mechanically meaningful drawbacks may refund points, but the combined refund is capped at **2 RP** per ancestry package. Purely narrative limitations do not refund points.
