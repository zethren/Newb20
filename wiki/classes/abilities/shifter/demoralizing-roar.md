# Demoralizing Roar

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Bear form), Tier 5

**Action:** Free Action.

Enemies in a 15-foot cone must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half the Shifter level + the Shifter's Constitution modifier) or become Shaken for 1d4 rounds. Shaken creatures take -2 on attack rolls, saves, and skill checks.
