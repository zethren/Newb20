# Precise Strike

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +1.

**Benefit:** Once per round, when you hit an enemy with a melee attack while a conscious ally who also has this feat threatens that enemy, you deal an extra 1d6 precision damage. This damage applies only while the enemy is flanked by you and that ally; see [Flanking](../game-mechanics.md#flanking).

**Required for:** None.
