# Flight Rules

This is the shared rules section for flight. The Aasimar's [Poor Flight feat](../feats/aasimar-wing-feats.md#poor-flight) provides the limited flight described here. These rules are provisional pending a complete treatment of aerial movement and maneuverability.

## Poor Flight

Poor Flight grants a 20-foot Fly speed and poor maneuverability. After each 5 feet flown, the flier may turn up to 45 degrees. It cannot turn in place. Turning uses the distance already flown; it does not cost additional movement.

## Good Flight

Good Flight is the reference for more agile flight. After each 5 feet flown, the flier may turn up to 90 degrees. It may also spend 5 feet of its movement to turn 90 degrees in place. Turning uses the listed movement; it does not cost additional movement.

While airborne using Poor Flight:

- The flier takes a -4 AB penalty.
- The flier takes a -2 penalty to Dodge AC.
- The flier takes a -2 penalty on Stealth checks.
- The flier takes a -2 penalty on Reflex saves.
- Casting any spell requires a Concentration check at DC 15 + the spell's level. On a failed check, the spell fails and its casting resource is expended.

These penalties apply while flying, not while descending safely with the Featherfall Wings feat.

## Attacking After Taking Damage

When a creature takes damage while airborne, it must attempt a [Fly](../skills/fly.md) check before its next attack while airborne to maintain control. The DC is **10 + the net HP damage from that hit after damage reduction**, following the mounted-combat damage stability formula. If the flier takes damage multiple times before that attack, it makes a separate Fly check for each damage instance. On a failed check, the flier loses control and begins falling.

## Falling and Regaining Flight

A falling creature descends 150 feet during the first round of its fall and 300 feet during each subsequent round. When it lands, it takes **1d6 damage per 10 feet fallen, to a maximum of 20d6**. A creature that takes damage from a fall lands Prone.

A creature with a Fly speed that is falling can use a Move Action to attempt a Fly check to regain control. The base DC is **10 + 1 for every 10 feet fallen (round up)**, to a maximum base DC of **30**. Apply environmental modifiers after the base DC cap. On a success, the creature regains control and stops falling. On a failure, it continues falling and can try again with another Move Action.

| Environmental condition | Fly DC modifier |
| --- | ---: |
| Light breeze | +1 |
| Heavy breeze | +3 |
| Light storm | +5 |
| Heavy storm | +10 |
