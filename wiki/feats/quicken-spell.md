# Quicken Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +4; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** You may cast a spell that normally takes a Standard Action as a Move Action. This does not change spells with a different casting time.

**Required for:** None.
