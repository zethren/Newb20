# Combat Reflexes

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13.

**Benefit:** You can take a number of Reactions each round equal to 1 + your Dexterity modifier (minimum 1). This replaces the normal limit of one Reaction. Your available Reactions refresh at the start of your turn. Each Attack of Opportunity and other Reaction uses one of these available Reactions.

**Required for:** None.
