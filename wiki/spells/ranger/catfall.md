# Catfall

| Spell detail | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 2nd |
| School | Transmutation |
| Class | Ranger |
| Casting Time | Reaction, when you fall |
| Range | Self |
| Components | V, S |
| Duration | Instantaneous |
| Attack | None |
| Save | None |
| Spell Resistance | No |

## Effect

You twist in the air and land on your feet. Reduce falling damage you take by 30, to a minimum of 0. If you land on a surface that can support you, you may immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking an opportunity attack from the creature whose action or effect caused the fall.
