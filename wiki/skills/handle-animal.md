# Handle Animal

**Key ability:** Charisma

Handle Animal covers directing, training, and calming animals. Specific training time and command procedures remain subject to the relevant class and creature rules.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Handle Animal grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Ride](ride.md) checks involving an animal the character handles or trains.
