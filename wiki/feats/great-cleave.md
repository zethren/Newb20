# Great Cleave

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Cleave; BAB +4.

**Benefit:** On your turn, when one of your melee attacks reduces a creature to 0 HP or kills it, you may make an extra melee attack with the same weapon against another creature within reach. If that attack also reduces its target to 0 HP or kills it, you may continue making extra attacks in this way. This replaces Cleave's once-per-turn limit; you can make no more than three extra attacks total per turn.

**Required for:** None.
