# Feline Sneak Attack

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Feline form)

The Feline deals precision damage when its target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC or when the Feline flanks the target with an ally.

| Feline tier | Primal Shifter level | Total sneak attack damage |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 | 1d6 |
| 3 | 8 | 3d6 |
| 5 | 16 | 5d6 |

These values are the Feline's total sneak attack dice, not additional dice gained at each tier. They apply while the Shifter is in Feline form. The progression is slower than a Rogue's 1d6 increase every other class level.

During an encounter, a target already engaged with the Feline cannot be made Flat-Footed by standard Stealth or Hide in Plain Sight (Wilderness). Flanking and physical conditions such as Blinded, Stunned, or Pinned remain valid triggers. Secondary natural attacks follow the global precision-damage rules in [Sneak Attack](../../../../game-mechanics/sneak-attack.md).
