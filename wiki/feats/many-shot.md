# Many Shot

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 17; BAB +6; [Improved Rapid Fire](improved-rapid-fire.md).

As a Standard Action, you can fire two arrows from a longbow or shortbow at one target within the weapon's range. You can also use this feat during a Full Attack Action, replacing any number of attacks in the routine with paired-arrow shots. Each paired-arrow shot uses one attack roll and takes a -4 AB penalty. On a hit, resolve damage from each arrow separately, applying Damage Reduction to each damage instance. Each paired-arrow shot is one attack, not two: apply precision damage and other effects that trigger once per hit only to its first arrow. You must have two arrows available for each paired-arrow shot.

**Required for:** None.
