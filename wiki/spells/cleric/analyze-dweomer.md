*7th-level Divination*

| Spell detail | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Level** | 7 |
| **School** | Divination |
| **Class** | Cleric |
| **Spell Lists** | Cleric 7th, Wizard 6th |
| **Casting Time** | Standard Action |
| **Range** | 30 feet |

## Effect

Study a magic item or active spell within 30 feet for 1 minute to learn its school, caster level, and one principal function; a second casting reveals another function.
