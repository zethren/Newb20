# Shake It Off

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisites:** None.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 Teamwork bonus on saving throws for each conscious ally within 5 feet who also has this feat, to a maximum bonus of +3. This bonus explicitly stacks with itself for each qualifying ally.

**Required for:** None.
