# Escape Artist

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Escape Artist covers slipping restraints, squeezing through tight spaces, and escaping physical confinement. It can replace MAB in the grapple and [Swallow Whole](../feats/swallow-whole.md) escape procedures described in [Grapple and Throw](../game-mechanics/grapple-and-throw.md).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Escape Artist grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Disable Device](disable-device.md) checks made to escape a mechanical restraint.
