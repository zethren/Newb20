# Ranger Combat Style Feats

These combat feats are available to any character who meets their prerequisites. A Ranger gains the feats of the Combat Style chosen at 2nd level at Ranger levels 2, 6, and 10, in order. The Ranger ignores the feats' ability-score and base-attack-bonus prerequisites when gaining them this way, but must gain each earlier feat in the progression first.
