# Fighter (Draft)

The Fighter is the durable, adaptable martial base class. Fighters are proficient with the broadest range of weapons and armor and specialize at character creation in a combat discipline.

## Class Statistics

| Statistic | Draft |
| --- | --- |
| Hit Die | d10 |
| Base attack bonus | High progression (+1 per level) |
| Good saves | Fortitude |
| Poor saves | Reflex, Will |
| Skill ranks per level | 2 + Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) |
| Class skills | [Athletics](../skills/athletics.md), [Handle Animal](../skills/handle-animal.md), [Persuasion (Intimidate)](../skills/persuasion.md), [Lore (History)](../skills/lore-history.md), [Perception](../skills/perception.md), [Ride](../skills/ride.md), [Survival](../skills/survival.md) |

## Proficiencies

Fighters are proficient with plate, leather, cloth, and robes. They are proficient with greatsword, greataxe, maul, longsword, shortsword, dagger, quarterstaff, club, mace, morningstar, spear, halberd, bardiche, longbow, shortbow, crossbow, heavy crossbow, sling, and throwing knives.

## Specializations

### Berserker

An aggressive front-line specialist who trades defense and restraint for a temporary surge in offense. The progression should include a controlled Rage feature, improved damage or maneuver pressure, and a high-level ability to remain dangerous while wounded. Rage duration, fatigue, and attribute effects remain open.

### Weapon Master

A specialist in a chosen weapon group. Gains increasingly reliable accuracy, damage, and tactical options with the chosen weapons, with later features expanding the group or granting a signature technique. Avoid unconditional bonuses that stack excessively with weapon enchantments.

### Defender

A durable protector who controls space, protects allies, and punishes enemies for moving past them. This path should complement, not duplicate, the [Bodyguard](bodyguard.md) prestige class and existing Defender feats.

### Cavalier

A mounted-combat specialist focused on coordinated charges, fighting from the saddle, and protecting or directing a mount. The progression should build on the shared [Mounted Combat rules](../game-mechanics/mounted-combat.md), the Ride skill, and mounted feats rather than replacing their universal rules.

| Specialization level | Cavalier feature concept |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Cavalry Training: Ride is a class skill; gain a bonded mount or equivalent mount access; reduce the standard mounted AB/AC penalty while riding a trained mount. |
| 5 | Mounted Momentum: improve the impact or maneuver effect of a mounted charge. |
| 9 | Saddle Guard: use the mount's position to protect an adjacent ally or redirect an attack, with a defined Reaction limit. |
| 13 | Coordinated Assault: make a mounted attack routine after a charge, subject to movement and mount-action limits. |
| 17 | Cavalry Command: grant nearby allied mounts or mounted allies a brief coordinated movement or attack benefit. |
| 20 | Master Cavalier: capstone improvement to mounted offense and defense; exact effect pending mount-rule balance. |

The bonded mount's progression, replacement, size, and independent action economy need rules. Cavalier features must be reconciled with mounted penalties, Ride checks, saddles, and the existing Mounted Combat feats.
