# Tail Combat

**Prerequisite:** [Prehensile Tail](prehensile-tail.md).

**Benefit:** You can wield a one-handed weapon or a shield with your tail. You must meet the normal requirements to use the weapon or shield; this feat does not grant proficiency. The tail does not grant additional actions or attacks.

**Required for:** None.
