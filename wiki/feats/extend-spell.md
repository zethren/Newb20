# Extend Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +1; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** Double the spell's duration. This does not affect instantaneous or permanent durations.

**Required for:** None.
