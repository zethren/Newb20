# Mounted Reflexes

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13; [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md).

An advanced mounted defense technique, you use your lightning reflexes to yank your steed out of harm's way, actively intercepting a blow meant for your mount.

**Action:** Reaction.

**Mechanic:** Once per round, when your mount is successfully hit by a physical melee or ranged attack, make a Ride check. If your check total meets or exceeds the enemy's final attack roll, you successfully manipulate your mount's footing, converting the strike into an automatic miss.

**Required for:** None.
