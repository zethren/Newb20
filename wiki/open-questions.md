# Rules QA

This document tracks unresolved rules, conflicts, and playtesting decisions. Resolve these items before treating the affected rules as final. Related player-facing pages should contain only the current rules, not design notes.

## Character Creation and Ancestries

- Define the character-creation order for base ability scores, ancestry modifiers, class features, forms, and later advancement.
- Define when ability-score advancement occurs and how those increases are applied.
- Name Elf subraces and resolve their ability adjustments, the proposed Constitution adjustment, and Enchantment Defense.
- Complete the remaining Kitsune details: shifting between fox, hybrid, and humanoid forms; form-change action and equipment rules; spirit magic; and spell-recall limits.
- Review Tengu Poor Flight against the shared flight penalties.
- Confirm the Hengeyokai form-change action cost and whether changing form can be interrupted.
- Compare Turtle, Raccoondog, and Raven form mechanics with Wolf, Feline, and Bear.
- Review the scaling value and interactions of ancestry traits.
- Review the Hengeyokai Shifter Talent feat's restriction to use in natural animal form.
- Define Spell Recall's maximum spell level, interaction with Sorcery Points, and balance with other spell-recovery effects.
- Resolve remaining tail and wing feat interactions.
- Review transformed ability-score totals and define mechanical uses for Intelligence and Charisma.

## Classes

- Complete and playtest the class drafts: verify class statistics, proficiencies, and feature progressions.
- Set the universal feat cadence and determine whether multiclassing needs specific anti-dip gates.
- Define Fighter specialization progressions and review Weapon Master's critical-profile interactions with general critical-hit rules.
- Define Rogue Talent effects, prerequisites, level requirements, action costs, and uses, including the long-range Sneak Attack talent.
- Define Ranger spell slots, preparation, spell list, and recovery. Review the [Stalker](classes/base-classes/ranger/specializations/stalker.md), [Beastmaster](classes/base-classes/ranger/specializations/beastmaster.md), and [Trapper](classes/base-classes/ranger/specializations/trapper.md) drafts; define animal companion stat blocks and advancement, the cadence and options for companion training feats (including ally rescue), and playtest trap uses, detection, and save DCs.
- Consider an alternate Ranger magic progression that starts spellcasting at 1st level and grants access to spells up to 6th level, like 5e, with Hunter's Mark as a signature Ranger spell rather than a class feature. Keep the current draft unchanged for now.
- Define the Druid's nature-associated weapon proficiencies, Druidic communication, and Wild Shape animal profiles and combat limits. Playtest the [Druid class](classes/base-classes/druid/index.md) and its [Land](classes/base-classes/druid/circles/land.md), [Moon](classes/base-classes/druid/circles/moon.md), [Shepherd](classes/base-classes/druid/circles/shepherd.md), and [Storm](classes/base-classes/druid/circles/storm.md) circles, including Storm's replacement features and Land's Renewal.
- Compare Circle of the Moon's expanded forms and Fluid Shifting with Primal Shifter form progression.
- Develop the Skinwalker concept as a possible prestige class open to Druids and other characters: a disciplined were-creature transformation combining aspects of Druid and Barbarian Rage. Define the transformation, Will save DC and duration when entering its Rage, [Berserk](game-mechanics/conditions.md#berserk) duration and recovery, and the monthly forced-transformation period, including the length and requirements of its nightly ritual.
- Define Berserk edge cases, including its save DC when forced by a class feature, how it ends if the nearest creature cannot be reached or attacked, and how to resolve equally near targets.
- Define Cleric domain spell lists and the relationship between a Cleric's deity or tradition and eligible domains.
- Define animal companion stat blocks, advancement, and default behavior for the Nature Domain, Shepherd Druid, and Beastmaster Ranger; set the cadence and options for companion training feats, including rescuing allies.
- Define Wizard class-feature levels and the effect of unassigned levels in the class progression tables.
- Define Wizard Enchant Item effects, activation, uses, and interaction with enchantment points.
- Define whether a bonded Staff must be carried or held to prepare or cast its extra spells.
- Define the Familiar's available forms, skill bonuses, possession range and duration, and spellcasting range and restrictions.
- Define Sorcerer Talent acquisition and the number of spell shapes/effects learned; define Innate Magic, Bloodline, and Sorcerous Mastery, and determine how Sorcerer spell shapes and effects combine with metamagic feats.
- Finalize the Sorcerer bloodline options, bloodline spells and features, and any costs or risks associated with Wild magic.
- Define Wizard Arcane Recovery, Arcane Bond advancement, and high-level mastery progression.
- Decide whether Sorcerer Overchannel or Short Rest recovery is available, and define any associated cost or sacrifice.
- Define Wizard Arcane Bond, including starting spellbook contents, spell-copying time and material costs, and whether repairing a Broken bonded Staff restores its bond.
- Define Transmuter's Stone benefits, capacity, and enchantment-point costs using the item-enchantment system.
- Playtest the consequences of a bonded Staff becoming Broken or a Familiar dying, including the provisional 4-point permanent Constitution damage.

## Skills and Recovery

- Identify trained-only skills and define how training is recorded.
- Define Concentration's spellcasting interactions.
- Define Heal check DCs and treatment rules for poisons, diseases, and other conditions, including Long-Term Care.
- Define Craft specialties and procedures, Perform specialties and effects, and Profession specialties and checks.
- Define Lore (Arcana) checks for identifying spells and the interaction of Will saves and True Seeing with Wizard Illusion features.
- Define maximum HP and baseline HP recovery during a Short or Long Rest.

## Combat Rules

- Define or remove the Swallow Whole prerequisite Improved Grapple; it currently has no rules page.
- Define ordinary Attack Bonus (AB) and distinguish AB from Base Attack Bonus (BAB) and Martial Ability Bonus (MAB).
- Define action-conversion limits, including whether a converted Standard Action can become multiple Free Actions and how Full-Round Actions interact with conversion.
- Define Reaction refresh timing and how effects that grant or consume Reactions interact with the normal limit.
- Define general combat-round procedure and a terminology glossary.
- Define damage resistance and immunity, including how Piercing Evocation and Elemental Mastery interact with them.
- Define manufactured-weapon critical profiles and general critical-hit resolution, including how Weapon Master's threat-range and multiplier improvements interact with other effects.
- Define the Dispel check and caster-level bonus caps, including whether Lesser Dispel is capped at +10 and Greater Dispel at +15, and whether a high-level Dispel can be uncapped.
- Define how Counterspell and Dispel identify a spell, when the reacting caster learns what is being cast, when the countering resource is expended, and when the original spell is interrupted.
- Define cantrip range and damage limits.
- Define which spells are native counters and how their eligibility is communicated to a reacting caster.
- Complete aerial movement and maneuverability rules: Fly speed and its interaction with movement, size, encumbrance, and forced movement; hovering, minimum movement, and stalls; collisions, landing, and other flight interruptions. Review flight-related attack, Dodge AC, Stealth, Reflex, and spellcasting modifiers against the completed combat rules.
- Define clearance and assistance rules for creatures of different sizes.

## Magic

- Complete the spell catalog and spellcasting rules, including divine spell slots, spell preparation, spell attacks, and save DCs.
- Verify the bonus spell-slot table from ability scores 30 onward and state the repeating progression.
- Decide whether the spell attack and saving-throw DC formulas are final rules or recommendations pending playtesting.
- Define the exponential Sorcery Point pool, damage augmentation, and the bonded Staff's bonus spell capacity through playtesting.
- Define scroll crafting and purchase prices, including the provisional Lore (Arcana) DC and caster-level scaling.

## Equipment and Crafting

- Complete the equipment catalog, prices, carrying capacity, and repair rules.
- Define magic-item creation costs, time, and checks. Playtest the proposed magic-item EP capacities, material modifiers, and enchantment costs across the weapon, armor, ring, clothing, boot, amulet, glove, and helmet tables. Define additional Legendary powers and Wizard Enchant Item's minor effects, activation, use limit, and interaction with permanent enchantments.
- Establish minimum market values for items eligible for Minor Alchemy.
- Define travel pace, foraging, weather, overland navigation, and general stealth rules.
