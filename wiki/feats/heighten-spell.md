# Heighten Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +1 to +8; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** Choose an increase from +1 to +8 when you apply this feat. The spell's effective level and save DC increase by that amount. The adjusted spell level cannot exceed 9.

**Required for:** None.
