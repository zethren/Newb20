# Natural Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

You can cast spells while in an animal shape. This feat works only while you are transformed into an animal; it does not let you cast spells in other forms or remove any other restriction imposed by the transformation.

**Required for:** None.
