# Perform

**Key ability:** Charisma

Perform covers presenting an artistic or entertaining performance before an audience.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Perform grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Persuasion](persuasion.md) checks to influence an audience through a performance.
