# Ranger Spell List

Ranger spells sharpen the hunt, aid travel through the wild, and let a hunter work with beasts and terrain. Wisdom is the Ranger's primary spellcasting ability. Spell save DCs follow the shared [spell rules](../spells.md#spell-attacks-and-save-dcs).

| --- | --- | --- |

| [Briarstep](../spells/ranger/briarstep.md) | Transmutation | Cross natural undergrowth quietly |
| [Cinderless Camp](../spells/ranger/cinderless-camp.md) | Abjuration | Conceal a small camp from casual notice |
| [Hunter's Scent](../spells/ranger/hunters-scent.md) | Divination | Read and follow a creature's trail |

| [Cloak of Needles](../spells/ranger/cloak-of-needles.md) | Abjuration | A close-fitting veil of spectral thorns surrounds you. | [Hound's Pursuit](../spells/ranger/hounds-pursuit.md) | Divination | Keep a quarry's trail through confusion |

On a hit, it deals an additional 2d6 magic damage plus 1d6 for every 4 caster levels after 3rd, and the attack's sound is muted; the target and impact make no audible noise beyond 10 feet until the start of your next turn. This spell does not remove other signs of the attack. |
