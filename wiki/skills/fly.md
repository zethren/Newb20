# Fly

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Fly covers controlling flight, maneuvering in the air, and maintaining control in difficult aerial conditions.

The current shared flight rules, including penalties for poor flight, are in [Flight Rules](../game-mechanics/flight.md).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Fly grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Acrobatics](acrobatics.md) checks made to maintain control during an aerial maneuver.
