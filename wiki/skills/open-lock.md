# Open Lock

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Open Lock covers opening or bypassing locks through fine manipulation. It does not by itself disable traps or other mechanical devices; see [Disable Device](disable-device.md).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Open Lock grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Disable Device](disable-device.md) checks involving locks or locking mechanisms.
