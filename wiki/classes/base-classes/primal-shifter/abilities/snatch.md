# Snatch

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Wolf form), Tier 2

When the Wolf hits with its primary Bite attack, it can make a free Grab or Trip check against the target's MAD without provoking attacks of opportunity.

Snatch bypasses the primary-weapon lock while the Wolf holds a victim: the Bite slot remains available for a full attack against that victim, including iterative bite and thrash attacks. See [Grapple, Thrash, and Throw](../../../../game-mechanics/grapple-and-throw.md).
