# Blood Frenzy

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Scent class feature; BAB +4.

The metallic aroma of deep trauma triggers a primal surge, sharpening your accuracy against wounded prey.

**Trigger:** The target must be below 50% of its maximum health and detectable within your scent radius.

**Benefit:** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all Attack Rolls (AB) and Martial Ability Bonus (MAB) checks against that target.

**Required for:** None.
