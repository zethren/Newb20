# Quietus Arrow

| Spell detail | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 2nd |
| School | Evocation |
| Class | Ranger |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range | Self |
| Components | V, S |
| Duration | Until the end of your turn |
| Attack | Ranged weapon attack |
| Save | None |
| Spell Resistance | Yes |

## Effect

The next ranged weapon attack you make before the spell ends is wrapped in a hush of force. On a hit, it deals an additional **2d6 magic damage plus 1d6 for every 4 caster levels after 3rd**, and the attack's sound is muted; the target and impact make no audible noise beyond 10 feet until the start of your next turn. This spell does not remove other signs of the attack.
