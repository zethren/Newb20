# Aasimar

An Aasimar combines a planar heritage with a base ancestry. It retains that ancestry's racial ability adjustments and Size package; the Aasimar heritage replaces the ancestry's other special traits.

Choose one resistance:

- **Resist Electric:** Energy Resistance 5 against electric damage.
- **Resist Acid:** Energy Resistance 5 against acid damage.

Every Aasimar also has wings. Flight and controlled descent require the [Aasimar Wing Feats](../feats/aasimar-wing-feats.md).

Energy Resistance follows the shared [Energy Resistance rule](../game-mechanics.md#energy-resistance).

The heritage also grants the following fixed Racial skill bonuses. The selected traits replace the base ancestry's other special traits.

| Base ancestry | Fixed skill bonuses |
| --- | --- |
| Human | +2 Heal, +2 Persuasion, +2 Perception, +2 Sense Motive |
| Elf | +2 Heal, +2 Perception, +2 Sense Motive, +2 Lore (Religion), +2 Stealth |
| Dwarf | +2 Heal, +2 Craft, +2 Perception, +2 Sense Motive, +2 Lore (Religion) |
| Half-Orc | +2 Heal, +2 Perception, +2 Persuasion |
| Halfling | +2 Heal, +2 Acrobatics, +2 Perception, +2 Sense Motive |
| Half-Elf | +2 Heal, +2 Perception, +2 Persuasion, +2 Sense Motive |
| Gnome | +2 Heal, +2 Lore (Religion) |
