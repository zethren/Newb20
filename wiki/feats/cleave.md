# Cleave

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Power Attack; BAB +1.

**Benefit:** Once per turn, when one of your melee attacks on your turn reduces a creature to 0 HP or kills it, you may immediately make one melee attack with the same weapon against another creature within reach. This extra attack does not consume an action and cannot trigger another Cleave.

**Required for:** [Great Cleave](great-cleave.md).
