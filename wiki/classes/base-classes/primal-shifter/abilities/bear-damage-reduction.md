# Bear Damage Reduction

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Bear form)

The Bear subtracts its DR value from each instance of physical blunt, piercing, or slashing damage. This DR cannot be bypassed by magic, alignment, or material types.

| Bear tier | Damage reduction |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | DR 1/- |
| 2 | DR 2/- |
| 3 | DR 5/- |
| 5 | DR 10/- |
