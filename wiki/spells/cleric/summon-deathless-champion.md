# Summon Deathless Champion

*9th-level Conjuration*

| Spell detail | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Level** | 9 |
| **School** | Conjuration |
| **Class** | Cleric |
| **Casting Time** | Standard Action |
| **Range** | 30 feet |
| **Components** | V, S, M (a humanoid bone fragment) |
| **Duration** | 1 minute |
| **Attack** | None |
| **Saving Throw** | None |
| **Spell Resistance** | No |

## Effect

Summon one Large undead champion in an unoccupied space within range. It has AC 19 and 150 hit points, moves up to 30 feet on its turn, and can make two melee attacks (+12 to hit, 2d10+6 slashing damage each). It acts immediately after you and vanishes when the spell ends or it is reduced to 0 hit points.
