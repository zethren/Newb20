# Mounted Combat

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ride 1 rank or BAB +1.

Your intensive training allows you to perfectly stabilize your center of gravity and synchronize your weapons output with your steed's movements.

**Benefit:** You completely and permanently eliminate the standard -2 AB / -2 AC mounted penalty when riding domesticated or standard mounts.

**Required for:** [Cavalry Synchronization](cavalry-synchronization.md), [Mounted Reflexes](mounted-reflexes.md), and [Savage Cavalry](savage-cavalry.md).
