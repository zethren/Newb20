# Spell Penetration

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Benefit:** Gain a +2 bonus on caster-level checks to overcome spell resistance.

**Required for:** [Improved Spell Penetration](improved-spell-penetration.md).
