# Lore (Religion)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Religion) covers religious traditions, deities, rites, religious organizations, and sacred symbols.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Religion) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Investigation](investigation.md) checks to interpret religious symbols or records.
