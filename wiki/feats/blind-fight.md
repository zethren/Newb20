# Blind-Fight

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** None.

**Benefit:** When a melee attack you make misses because of a concealment miss chance, you may reroll that miss chance once. You must use the second result. This does not reveal or locate a concealed or unseen creature.

**Required for:** [Primal Awareness](primal-awareness.md).
