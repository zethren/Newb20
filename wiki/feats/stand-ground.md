# Stand Ground

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +3.

You transform your threatened area into a hard kinetic denial zone, physically stopping enemies from slipping past you to reach your allies.

**Mechanic:** When an enemy creature provokes an Attack of Opportunity from you by moving out of or through your threatened grid squares, you can choose to execute a **Martial Ability Check (MAB)** vs. the target's static **Martial Ability Defense (MAD)** in place of a standard damage strike.

**Success:** If your MAB check meets or exceeds the target's MAD, you successfully intercept their momentum. The target takes 0 damage, but their base movement speed is instantly reduced to 0 feet for the remainder of their turn, locking them in place exactly where you struck them.

**Required for:** None.
