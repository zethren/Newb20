# Misdirection

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raccoondog form), Tier 2

**Action:** Standard Action.

Choose one creature within 30 feet that can see or hear you. Make a MAB check against its MAD. On a success, it becomes Flat-Footed against your next attack before the end of your next turn.
