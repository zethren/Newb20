# Shell Fortress

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Turtle form), Tier 5

**Action:** Standard Action.

Until the start of your next turn, you brace inside your shell and gain DR 10/-, a +8 racial bonus to MAD against Trip, Bull Rush, Drag, and Reposition maneuvers, and immunity to forced movement. While braced, your speed is 0, you cannot attack, and you cannot take Reactions. This replaces Shell Guard's benefits; they do not stack.
