# Combat Casting

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 Supernatural bonus on [Concentration](../skills/concentration.md) checks.

**Required for:** [Improved Combat Casting](improved-combat-casting.md).
