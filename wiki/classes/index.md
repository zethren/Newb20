# Classes

## Base Classes

- [Fighter](base-classes/fighter/index.md): a durable weapon-and-armor combatant with combat specializations.
- [Barbarian](base-classes/barbarian/index.md): a durable martial combatant who channels Rage and develops Rage Talents.
- [Rogue](base-classes/rogue/index.md): a skill-focused infiltrator who builds a specialty by choosing Rogue Talents.
- [Ranger](base-classes/ranger/index.md): an apex hunter and wilderness tracker with Stalker, Beastmaster, and Trapper specializations.
- [Paladin](base-classes/paladin/index.md): a lawful-good champion with divine resilience and a choice between a bonded mount or weapon.
- [Monk](base-classes/monk/index.md): a lawful martial artist who channels ki through unarmed strikes and monk weapons.
- [Cleric](base-classes/cleric/index.md): a divine spellcaster who channels divine energy and chooses two alignment-appropriate domains; see [Cleric Domains](base-classes/cleric/domains.md).
- [Druid](base-classes/druid/index.md): a versatile nature spellcaster who supports nature-themed allies through utility, animal companions, elemental magic, and Wild Shape.
- [Wizard](base-classes/wizard/index.md): an Intelligence-based prepared arcane caster who chooses a school and bonds with a staff or familiar.
- [Sorcerer](base-classes/sorcerer/index.md): a Charisma-based spontaneous caster whose magic comes from a bloodline.
- [Primal Shifter](base-classes/primal-shifter/index.md): a martial shapeshifter with six animal-form progressions.
- [Primal Shifter form profiles](base-classes/primal-shifter/index.md#form-profiles): Wolf, Feline, Bear, Turtle, Raccoondog, and Raven.

## Prestige Classes

Prestige classes use a 10-level progression.

- [Bodyguard](prestige-classes/bodyguard/index.md): a martial protector who redirects or absorbs damage for a designated ally.

## Full Spell-Level Progression

Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric, and Druid use the standard d20 full-caster spell-level unlock schedule:

| Class level | Highest spell level |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1-2 | 1 |
| 3-4 | 2 |
| 5-6 | 3 |
| 7-8 | 4 |
| 9-10 | 5 |
| 11-12 | 6 |
| 13-14 | 7 |
| 15-16 | 8 |
| 17+ | 9 |

10th-level spells are reserved for epic magic. Spell slots or point budgets, spells known or prepared, casting attributes, and recovery are class-specific. Cantrips are minor at-will spells and do not use spell slots or Sorcery Points.

Base-class progression tables use the standard d20 columns for level, BAB, Fortitude, Reflex, Will, and class features. BAB includes iterative attacks when applicable; good and poor saves use the same progression as the Bodyguard table.
