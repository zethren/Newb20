# Beast Shape III

| Spell detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 5 |
| School | Transmutation (Polymorph) |
| Class | Druid |
| Casting time | Standard Action |
| Range | Self |
| Components | V, S, M (a sample or tuft from the chosen creature) |
| Duration | 1 minute/CL |
| Spell attack | None |
| Saving throw | None |
| Spell resistance | No |

## Effect

This spell functions as [Beast Shape II](beast-shape-ii.md), and also allows Diminutive or Huge animal forms and Small or Medium magical-beast forms.

| Form size and type | Physical ability adjustment | Natural Armor bonus to Natural Armor AC |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Diminutive animal | +6 Size bonus to Dexterity, -4 Size penalty to Strength | +1 |
| Huge animal | +6 Size bonus to Strength, -4 Size penalty to Dexterity | +6 |
| Small magical beast | +4 Size bonus to Dexterity | +2 |
| Medium magical beast | +4 Size bonus to Strength | +4 |

The form may grant burrow 30 feet, climb 90 feet, fly 90 feet (good maneuverability), swim 90 feet, blindsense 30 feet, darkvision 60 feet, low-light vision, or scent if the chosen creature has that ability. It may also grant constrict, ferocity, grab, jet, poison, pounce, rake, trample, trip, or web when present in the chosen profile.
