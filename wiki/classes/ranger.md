# Ranger (Draft)

The Ranger is a self-sufficient ranged combatant and wilderness specialist. Rangers combine weapon accuracy with scouting, tracking, trapping, and optional animal partnership. The Ranger remains a separate base class from the Thief.

Hunter's Mark, Combat Style, and the level-scaled features above are concepts pending exact mechanics. Ranger features should emphasize scouting, target selection, and sustained pursuit without duplicating Thief Sneak Attack or Fighter weapon mastery.

## Specializations

- **Archer:** Improves ranged weapon accuracy, positioning, and control. Should reward prepared shots and target selection without making close-range archery universally optimal.
- **Beastmaster:** Gains a trained animal companion that scouts and fights alongside the Ranger. Define companion actions, advancement, replacement, and how its turns interact with the shared action economy.
- **Bounty Hunter (Trapper):** Specializes in pursuit, prepared traps, and capturing or disabling a chosen target. Define setup time, detection, saves, and trap persistence.
