# Bear Form

The Bear form is the Primal Shifter's guardian and tank profile. Its shifter modifiers are added to the [global size modifiers](../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md).

| Tier | Character level | Size | STR | DEX | CON | Natural armor | Form feature |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Medium | +0 | +0 | +2 | +3 | DR 1/-; Scent 30 ft. |
| 2 | 4 | Large | +2 | -2 | +6 | +6 | DR 2/-; [Guardian's Intercept](../abilities/shifter/guardians-intercept.md) |
| 3 | 8 | Large | +2 | -2 | +8 | +8 | DR 5/-; [Bear's Maneuvering Stance](../abilities/shifter/bear-maneuvering-stance.md) |
| 4 | 12 | Large | +2 | -2 | +10 | +10 | [Persistent Shifting](../abilities/shifter/persistent-shifting.md) |
| 5 | 16 | Huge | +4 | -4 | +12 | +12 | DR 10/-; [Demoralizing Roar](../abilities/shifter/demoralizing-roar.md) |
| 6 | 20 | Huge | +4 | -4 | +16 | +16 | [Swift Shifting](../abilities/shifter/swift-shifting.md) |

## Natural Attacks

The Bear's main-hand attack is Claw 1; its off-hand attacks are Claw 2 and Bite. Damage dice and slot properties follow [Combat Scaling](../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md).

| Weapon slot | Damage row | Medium (Tier 1) | Large (Tiers 2-4) | Huge (Tiers 5-6) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claw 1 (main hand) | Heavy attacks | 1d6 + 1.0x STR | 1d8 + 1.0x STR | 2d6 + 1.0x STR |
| Claw 2 (off hand) | Heavy attacks | 1d6 + 0.5x STR | 1d8 + 0.5x STR | 2d6 + 0.5x STR |
| Bite (off hand) | Heavy attacks | 1d8 + 1.5x STR | 2d6 + 1.5x STR | 3d6 + 1.5x STR |

See the [Primal Shifter](../primal-shifter.md) for class progression and [Shifting Talent](../abilities/shifter/shifting-talent.md) for form-tier unlocks.
