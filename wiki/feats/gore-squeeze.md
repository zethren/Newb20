# Gore Squeeze

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Large size category or larger; Snatch or Snag capability.

You use raw, suffocating kinetic pressure to crush the life out of a target while systematically warping and buckling their equipped armor plating.

**Action:** Standard Action.

**Target:** You must currently have a target secured in a persistent grapple or snatch state.

**Check:** Roll an offensive MAB against the target's MAD.

On a success, the victim takes standard natural-weapon squeeze damage (per the safe-path grapple rules), and the squeeze damage is also applied directly to the opponent's equipped armor or held weapon. The item subtracts its flat Hardness from the damage, and the remainder is deducted from its structural Hit Points. If the item is magical, damage can reduce it to a minimum of 1 HP (triggering the Broken condition), but it cannot be reduced to 0 HP or permanently destroyed.

**Required for:** None.
