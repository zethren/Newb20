# Disguise

**Key ability:** Charisma

Disguise changes a character's appearance, mannerisms, or presentation to resemble another person or pass as a different kind of creature.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Disguise grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Persuasion](persuasion.md) checks to maintain a cover identity under questioning.
