# Lore (Arcana)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Arcana) represents learned knowledge of arcane traditions, magical theory, and arcane phenomena. It includes the former Knowledge (Arcana) and Spellcraft knowledge uses.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Arcana) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Use Magic Device](use-magic-device.md) checks involving arcane items.
