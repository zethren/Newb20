# Primal Awareness

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Scent class feature; [Blind-Fight](blind-fight.md).

Your primal instincts let you translate scent data and microscopic acoustic cues into a physical map, rendering physical invisibility ineffective once you have located a target.

**Benefit:** Once a successful Sniff check flags an unseen target's exact location, you ignore the 50% total-concealment miss chance and your melee natural weapons strike as if the target were fully visible. You also retain your dynamic Uncanny Dodge protection against melee attacks by that target while it remains in your active Scent tracking field.

**Required for:** None.
