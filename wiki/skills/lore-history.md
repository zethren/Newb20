# Lore (History)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (History) covers significant events, historical figures, past cultures, and the interpretation of historical records.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (History) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Investigation](investigation.md) checks to research or interpret historical evidence.
