# Aasimar Wing Feats

These feats provide increasing control over an Aasimar's wings. Neither feat is granted automatically by the ancestry.

## Featherfall Wings

**Prerequisite:** Aasimar heritage with wings.

**Benefit:** Once per Short Rest, when you fall, you can use a reaction to spread your wings and descend safely. You descend at up to 60 feet per round and take no damage from the fall. This does not let you gain altitude or fly.

## Poor Flight

**Prerequisite:** Featherfall Wings.

**Benefit:** You can fly using your wings with poor maneuverability. Your Fly speed is 20 feet. While airborne, you take a -4 AB penalty, a -2 penalty to Dodge AC, a -2 penalty on Stealth checks, and a -2 penalty on Reflex saves. Casting any spell while airborne requires a Concentration check at DC 15 + the spell's level; failure causes the spell to fail and its casting resource to be expended.

Flight handling and the full effects of poor maneuverability are provisional and are defined further in the [Flight Rules](../game-mechanics/flight.md).
