# Spring Attack

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13; Dodge; Mobility; BAB +4.

**Benefit:** You can take a Full Attack Action as a Standard Action. You may divide the movement from your Move Action before, between, and after the attacks in that Full Attack, up to your normal movement for that Move Action. Resolve movement-triggered Attacks of Opportunity normally.

**Required for:** None.
