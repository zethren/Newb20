# Greater Weapon Focus

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** BAB +8; [Weapon Focus](weapon-focus.md) with the chosen weapon.

Choose the same weapon for which you have Weapon Focus. Your Weapon Focus bonus with that weapon increases to +2 total.

**Required for:** None.
