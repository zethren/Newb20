# Perception

**Key ability:** Wisdom

Perception covers noticing sounds, sights, scents, and other sensory details. It combines Spot and Listen and is used to detect observable signs before examining them in detail.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Perception grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Investigation](investigation.md) checks to examine a clue the character has noticed.
