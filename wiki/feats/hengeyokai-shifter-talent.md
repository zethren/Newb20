# Hengeyokai Shifter Talent

**Prerequisites:** Hengeyokai of an eligible animal type; Primal Shifter level 1; the matching form track is below its maximum tier for your current class level.

**Benefit:** Immediately gain one additional Shifter Talent selection. Advance the Primal Shifter form matching your Hengeyokai animal type by one tier. This bonus selection cannot raise the form above the maximum tier allowed by your Primal Shifter level. It does not replace or delay a Shifter Talent granted by class progression.

| Hengeyokai type | Matching Primal Shifter form |
| --- | --- |
| Wolf (Wolf) | Wolf |
| Neko (Cat) | Feline |
| Kappa (Turtle) | Turtle |
| Tanuki (Raccoondog) | Raccoondog |
| Bear (Bear) | Bear |
| Tengu (Raven) | Raven |

Kitsune are a separate spirit classification and do not qualify. The feat's benefit may need to be restricted to use while shifted into the Hengeyokai's natural animal form after balance review.
