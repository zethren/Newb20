# Conditioning

**Class:** Fighter 5, 10, 15

Conditioning improves the Fighter's resistance to the Combat Stamina mechanics described in [Resting and Combat Stamina](../../../../game-mechanics/resting.md).

| Fighter level | Effect |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | Treat the Fighter's Constitution score as 4 higher than normal solely when calculating Rounds to Fatigue and Rounds to Exhaustion. |
| 10 | The bonus above increases to 8. The Fighter no longer suffers the Strength and Dexterity penalty from the [Fatigued](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#fatigued) condition; its other restrictions still apply. |
| 15 | The bonus above increases to 12. The Fighter no longer suffers the Strength and Dexterity penalty from the [Exhausted](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#exhausted) condition; its movement restriction still applies. A Short Rest removes the Fighter's Exhausted condition if Combat Stamina accumulation was its only cause. |

This Constitution increase applies only to the Combat Stamina thresholds above. It does not change the Fighter's Constitution score, modifier, or any other Constitution-based value.
