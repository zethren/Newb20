# Rogue Talents

You gain one Rogue Talent at every even Rogue level from 2nd through 20th. Unless a talent says otherwise, you can select it only once.

## Combat and Defense Talents

| Talent | Prerequisite | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evasion | Rogue 2 | Avoid or reduce harm from effects that allow a Reflex save. |
| Uncanny Dodge | Rogue 2 | Retain defensive awareness when surprised or attacked by an unseen foe. |
| Improved Evasion | Rogue 8, Evasion | Improve Evasion so a successful Reflex save avoids the effect and a failed save reduces it. |
| Improved Uncanny Dodge | Rogue 8, Uncanny Dodge | Prevent enemies from gaining an advantage by flanking the Rogue. |
| Opportunist | Rogue 6 | Make a limited reaction attack when an enemy nearby is struck by an ally. |
| Defensive Roll | Rogue 8 | Reduce the damage of one especially dangerous attack once per encounter. |
| Slippery Mind | Rogue 10 | Gain another chance to resist certain mind-affecting effects. |
| Improved Opportunist | Rogue 14, Opportunist | Improve the Rogue's Opportunist reaction. |
| Perfect Opening | Rogue 20 | Turn a decisive opening into a powerful precision strike. |

## Sneak Attack Talents

These talents let you specialize in greater precision damage or in debilitating effects. You can select talents from either path or combine them, subject to their prerequisites.

### Strike Talent Rules

A strike talent can be applied when you hit a Flanked or Flat-Footed target, but only if the attack deals at least 1 damage to that target after Damage Reduction. Secondary and off-hand attacks can qualify even when they do not deal Sneak Attack damage dice. For each qualifying hit, you can apply any number of distinct strike talents you know and meet the prerequisites for; each talent can be applied once per hit. Resolve each talent's saving throw separately. Repeated applications stack as described in the talent. Unless a talent states otherwise, the target attempts a Fortitude save against a DC of **10 + half your Rogue level (rounded down) + your Dexterity modifier**.
