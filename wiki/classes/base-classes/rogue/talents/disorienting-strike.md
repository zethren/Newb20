# Disorienting Strike

**Class:** Rogue Talent  
**Prerequisite:** Rogue 8, Hindering Strike, Sneak Attack 4d6

When a qualifying hit deals at least 1 damage to the target after Damage Reduction, you can make the target attempt a Fortitude save against your Sneak Attack Talent DC. On a failed save, the target takes a **-2 penalty on attack rolls and Reflex saves for 1 round**.

Each additional failed save from this talent adds another -2 penalty while its effect lasts. Track each application separately; each penalty ends after its own 1-round duration.
