# Opportunist

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +4.

**Benefit:** Once per round, when a conscious ally who also has this feat hits an enemy with a melee attack while you threaten that enemy, you may spend a Reaction to make one melee attack against it. This attack is an Attack of Opportunity and is resolved after the triggering attack.

**Required for:** None.
