# Half-Orc

- **Powerful:** +2 Racial bonus to Strength.
- **Hardy:** +2 Racial bonus to Constitution.
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft.
- **Poor Scent:** Scent range is 15 ft for passive detection and active tracking. See [Scent and Tracking](../game-mechanics/scent.md).
- **Weapon Familiarity:** Proficient with greataxes, falchions, and weapons with "Orc" in their name. This proficiency prevents the -4 AB penalty for using an improvised weapon.
