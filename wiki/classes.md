# Classes

## Base Classes

- [Fighter](classes/fighter.md): a durable weapon-and-armor combatant; draft specializations include Berserker, Weapon Master, Defender, and Cavalier.
- [Rogue](classes/rogue.md): a skill-focused infiltrator with Assassin, Burglar, and Swashbuckler specializations.
- [Ranger](classes/ranger.md): a ranged and wilderness specialist with Archer, Beastmaster, and Bounty Hunter specializations.
- [Cleric](classes/cleric.md): a divine spellcaster whose domain reflects their faith and calling.
- [Druid](classes/druid.md): a nature-centered spellcaster with a circle specialization and Wild Shape.
- [Wizard](classes/wizard.md): an Intelligence-based prepared arcane caster who chooses a school and bonds with a staff or familiar.
- [Sorcerer](classes/sorcerer.md): a Charisma-based spontaneous caster whose magic comes from a bloodline.
- [Primal Shifter](classes/primal-shifter.md): a martial shapeshifter with six animal-form progressions.
- [Primal Shifter form profiles](classes/primal-shifter.md#form-profiles): Wolf, Feline, Bear, Turtle, Raccoondog, and Raven.

## Prestige Classes

Prestige classes use a 10-level progression.

- [Bodyguard](classes/bodyguard.md): a martial protector who redirects or absorbs damage for a designated ally.

## Full Spell-Level Progression

Wizard, Sorcerer, Cleric, and Druid use the standard d20 full-caster spell-level unlock schedule:

| Class level | Highest spell level |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1-2 | 1 |
| 3-4 | 2 |
| 5-6 | 3 |
| 7-8 | 4 |
| 9-10 | 5 |
| 11-12 | 6 |
| 13-14 | 7 |
| 15-16 | 8 |
| 17+ | 9 |

Player characters do not currently gain 10th-level spells; those are reserved for epic magic. Spell slots or point budgets, spells known or prepared, casting attributes, and recovery are class-specific. Cantrips are minor at-will magic and do not use spell slots or Sorcery Points; damaging cantrips should remain short-range and low-damage.

Base-class progression tables use the standard d20 columns for level, BAB, Fortitude, Reflex, Will, and class features. BAB includes iterative attacks when applicable; good and poor saves use the same progression as the Bodyguard table. The base-class entries are first-pass drafts. Detailed feature text, Sorcerer talent advancement, divine spell-slot progression, subclass advancement, and balance still require development.
