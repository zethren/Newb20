# Wizard Spell List

An original Wizard catalog of familiar d20 spell concepts across all eight schools. Each spell links to its rules. Wizards may learn from any school; level access, spellbook, and preparation are described on the [Wizard class page](../classes/base-classes/wizard/index.md#spellcasting).
