# Shared Resolve

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisites:** None.

While you are within 5 feet of at least one conscious ally who also has this feat, you gain a +1 Teamwork bonus on saving throws. This bonus increases by +1 for each additional adjacent ally with this feat, to a maximum of +3.

**Required for:** None.
