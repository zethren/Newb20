# Mobility

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13; Dodge.

**Benefit:** You gain a +4 Circumstance bonus to Dodge AC against Attacks of Opportunity provoked by your movement. See [Attacks of Opportunity from Movement](../game-mechanics.md#attacks-of-opportunity-from-movement).

**Required for:** [Spring Attack](spring-attack.md).
