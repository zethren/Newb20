# Raven Form

The Raven form is the Primal Shifter's aerial scout and skirmisher. Tengu Hengeyokai use this form track. Its form modifiers are added to the [global size modifiers](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md).

| Tier | Character level | Size | STR | DEX | CON | Natural armor | Form feature |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Medium | +0 | +2 | +0 | +1 | Low-light vision; Poor Flight 20 ft. |
| 2 | 4 | Large | +2 | +4 | +2 | +2 | [Talon Snatch](../abilities/talon-snatch.md); Poor Flight 30 ft. |
| 3 | 8 | Large | +2 | +6 | +2 | +3 | [Raven's Eye](../abilities/ravens-eye.md); Poor Flight 40 ft. |
| 4 | 12 | Large | +2 | +8 | +2 | +4 | Good Flight 40 ft. |
| 5 | 16 | Huge | +4 | +10 | +4 | +5 | [Swooping Assault](../abilities/swooping-assault.md); Good Flight 50 ft. |
| 6 | 20 | Huge | +4 | +12 | +4 | +7 | Good Flight 60 ft.; [Swift Shifting](../abilities/swift-shifting.md) |

Poor and Good Flight use the shared [Flight Rules](../../../../game-mechanics/flight.md). Poor Flight's airborne penalties apply at tiers 1-3. Flight speed and maneuverability at tiers 4-6 follow the Good Flight rules.

## Natural Attacks

Damage uses the Raven's current Size column in the [weapon damage by size table](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size), starting from the Medium base damage below. The beak uses the Bite slot's Strength modifier and damage types; talon slot properties and damage types follow [Combat Scaling](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#natural-weapon-properties).

| Weapon slot | Medium base damage | Damage types |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Beak (main hand) | 1d4 + 1.5x STR | Blunt, piercing, and slashing |
| Talon 1 (off hand) | 1d4 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |
| Talon 2 (off hand) | 1d4 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |

See [Primal Shifter](../index.md) and [Shifting Talent](../abilities/shifting-talent.md) for selection and tier rules.
