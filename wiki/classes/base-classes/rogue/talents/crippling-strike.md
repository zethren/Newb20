# Crippling Strike

**Class:** Rogue Talent  
**Prerequisite:** Rogue 8, Hindering Strike, Sneak Attack 4d6

When a qualifying hit deals at least 1 damage to the target after Damage Reduction, you can make the target attempt a Fortitude save against your Sneak Attack Talent DC. On a failed save, the target takes **1 point of temporary Strength ability damage**.

Each additional failed save from this talent deals another point of Strength ability damage. This reduces the target's Strength score; recalculate its Strength modifier and related values. The damage recovers according to the normal rest rules for ability damage.
