# Acrobatics

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Acrobatics covers balance, tumbling, controlled movement, and other feats of agility, including the uses formerly handled by Balance, Tumble, and Mobility.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Acrobatics grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Athletics](athletics.md) checks to jump or land safely.

## Rank Benefit

At 10 ranks, gain a +1 Circumstance bonus to Dodge AC. This bonus increases to +2 at 20 ranks. It is removed whenever the character loses their Dodge AC component; see [Armor Class Components](../game-mechanics/armor-class.md).
