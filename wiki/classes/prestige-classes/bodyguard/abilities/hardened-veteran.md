# Hardened Veteran

**Class:** Bodyguard 4; also called Greater Warden's Watch

When the Bodyguard absorbs half of a Protected Charge's damage with Warden's Watch, reduce the damage the Bodyguard takes from that intercepted portion by the Bodyguard's class level plus their full Constitution modifier. Apply this reduction separately to each intercepted portion. The Bodyguard takes a minimum of 1 damage from each intercepted portion; the charge's damage is unchanged.
