# Health, Dying, and Recovery

## Death and Dying

- At **0 HP**, a creature is **Unconscious**.
- Below **0 HP**, a creature is **Dying** and loses **1 HP at the end of each of its turns**.
- A creature dies when its HP is at or below negative its current Constitution score.

## Healing

Healing cannot raise a creature above its maximum HP.

## Stabilizing

A successful Heal check can stabilize a Dying creature, bringing it to **1 HP** and ending its loss of 1 HP per turn. A creature stabilized this way remains Unconscious. It wakes when it receives further healing or completes a Short Rest.

See [Heal](../skills/heal.md) for treatment and check procedures.
