# Point Blank Shot

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** None.

**Benefit:** You gain a +1 Supernatural bonus to AB and AD with ranged weapon attacks against targets within 30 feet. These attacks do not provoke Attacks of Opportunity from enemies whose reach you are within; see [Ranged Attacks in Melee](../game-mechanics.md#ranged-attacks-in-melee).

**Required for:** [Snipe](snipe.md).
