# Improved Spell Penetration

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Spell Penetration](spell-penetration.md); caster level 4.

**Benefit:** Gain an additional +2 bonus on caster-level checks to overcome spell resistance, for a total bonus of +4.

**Required for:** [Greater Spell Penetration](greater-spell-penetration.md).
