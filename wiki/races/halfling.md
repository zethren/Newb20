# Halfling

Halflings are Small. Apply the Small adjustments and effects from [Combat Scaling](../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-modifiers), including its ability, attack, AC, maneuver, Stealth, reach, movement, weapon-damage, ration, weight, and space rules.

- **Low-Light Vision:** See in dim light better than humans do.
- **Stealth:** +2 Racial bonus to Stealth checks.
- **Halfling Luck:** +1 Racial bonus on all saving throws and all skill checks.

Racial bonuses from these traits do not stack with one another; use the highest bonus to a given check under the [bonus stacking rules](../attributes.md#bonus-types-and-stacking). Thus, the racial Stealth bonus remains +2; fear saves receive the +1 bonus from Halfling Luck. The distinct +4 Size bonus to Stealth stacks with the +2 Racial bonus.
