# Wizard

Wizards are Intelligence-based arcane spellcasters who learn magic through study and prepare spells from a spellbook. At 1st level, each Wizard chooses an Arcane School and an Arcane Bond; these are separate choices.

## Spellcasting

The Wizard prepares arcane spells from a spellbook. Intelligence governs Wizard spellcasting. To cast or prepare a spell, the Wizard must have an Intelligence score of at least 10 + the spell's level. The Wizard regains expended spell slots after a Long Rest and prepares spells from the spellbook afterward.

Spell attack bonuses and spell save DCs follow the shared [spell attack and save DC rules](../../../spells.md#spell-attacks-and-save-dcs).

See the [Wizard spell list](../../../spell-lists/wizard.md) for spells that can be learned in a spellbook and the individual spell rules.

Any spellcaster can use the shared [Counterspell rules](../../../spells.md#counterspell).

### Spellbook and Daily Preparation

At the start of each day, after a Long Rest, the Wizard prepares a list of spells from their spellbook. The number of distinct spells prepared equals the Wizard's class level plus their Intelligence modifier, to a minimum of one spell. A prepared spell can be of any spell level the Wizard can currently cast, as long as it is recorded in the spellbook. The preparation limit is separate from the number of spell slots: when casting a prepared spell, the Wizard expends a spell slot of that spell's level. A prepared spell may be cast more than once if the Wizard has additional spell slots of its level.

Cantrips known by the Wizard are always prepared, do not count against this limit, and can be cast at will without expending spell slots.

## Scribe Scroll

At 1st level, the Wizard gains Scribe Scroll. To copy a Wizard spell from a spell scroll or another spellbook, make a Lore (Arcana) check against a DC of 15 + the spell's level. On a success, the spell is recorded in the Wizard's spellbook. The source scroll is consumed only when the copy succeeds; copying from another spellbook does not remove or damage the source. A copied spell that is above the Wizard's current spell-level access cannot be prepared or cast until the Wizard can cast spells of that level.

The Wizard can also create a scroll of a spell they have recorded and can cast. The spell must be prepared, and creating the scroll expends a spell slot of its level. The Wizard chooses a caster level from the minimum needed to cast the spell up to their Wizard level. Fine parchment, special inks, and other materials are required. Crafting costs **25 gp × spell level × caster level**. Treat a cantrip as a 1st-level spell for pricing. Creating a scroll takes one day.

## Arcane School

Choose one school at character creation. School features should develop at Wizard levels 1, 3, 6, 10, 14, and 18. The initial choices are:

| School | Specialty |
| --- | --- |
| Abjuration | Wards, barriers, dispelling, and protection |
| Conjuration | Summoning, creation, and teleportation |
| Divination | Detection, hidden knowledge, and foresight |
| Enchantment | Influencing emotion, behavior, and thought |
| Evocation | Energy, force, light, and direct damage |
| Illusion | Deception through false sensory impressions |
| Necromancy | Life force, death, and undeath |
| Transmutation | Altering form, matter, and physical properties |

### Specialization Rules

**Recommendation:** A specialist keeps access to all eight schools and receives no extra spell slots. Their school feature progression is the specialization benefit; this avoids adding another resource bonus to the Wizard's spell slots and Arcane Bond capacity. School features are drafted at Wizard levels 1, 3, 6, 10, 14, and 18.

### Arcane School Feature DC

An Arcane School feature that calls for a save uses a school feature DC of **10 + half the Wizard's level (round down) + Intelligence modifier**.

## Arcane Bond: Staff or Familiar

At 1st level, choose one Arcane Bond: a bonded Staff or a Familiar. This is an additional Wizard feature and is separate from the Arcane School choice.

### Bonded Staff

The bonded Staff grants a pool of extra prepared spell capacity equal to one spell at the Wizard's highest available spell level. Lower-level spells cost half as much capacity for each level below that maximum. The Wizard can distribute this capacity among extra prepared spell slots in any combination, provided the total cost does not exceed the pool. Each extra prepared spell is castable once and expended like a normal spell slot; the capacity returns after a Short Rest.

The Wizard can bond with a different Staff by performing a 24-hour ritual. This bond grants the Staff the Wizard's Arcane Bond benefits without consuming any of the Staff's enchantment points. If the bonded Staff gains the [Broken condition](../../../game-mechanics/item-durability.md), the Wizard suffers **4 points of permanent Constitution damage**. This damage recovers through Long Rests, with possible additional recovery from [Long-Term Care](../../../skills/heal.md), as described in the [health recovery rules](../../../game-mechanics/health.md).

| Spell level | Capacity cost per extra prepared spell |
| ---: | ---: |
| Highest available | 1 |
| One level below highest | 1/2 |
| Two levels below highest | 1/4 |
| Three levels below highest | 1/8 |
| Each additional level below | Half the previous cost |

For example, at 9th-level spell access, the Staff can prepare one 9th-level spell, two 8th-level spells, four 7th-level spells, or any combination with equivalent total capacity. The same rule applies at lower maximum spell levels.

### Familiar

The Familiar is a bound fae creature that takes the form of a small animal. It grants passive skill bonuses, can communicate telepathically with the Wizard, and can be possessed and controlled by the Wizard. As a Free Action, the Wizard can command the Familiar. While possessing the Familiar, the Wizard enters a trance-like state; the Wizard's body remains where it was and is [Helpless](../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#helpless).

The Wizard can cast spells through the Familiar, using the Familiar as the spell's point of origin. These spells use the Wizard's actions, not the Familiar's; a spell that takes a Full-Round Action consumes the Wizard's Standard and Move Actions.

If the Familiar dies, the Wizard must resummon it through a 24-hour ritual. The ritual time can be accumulated across multiple sittings. The Wizard suffers **4 points of permanent Constitution damage** when the Familiar dies. This damage recovers through Long Rests, with possible additional recovery from [Long-Term Care](../../../skills/heal.md), as described in the [health recovery rules](../../../game-mechanics/health.md).
