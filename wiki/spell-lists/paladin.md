# Paladin Spell List

Paladins prepare divine spells according to their class progression. Charisma is the Paladin's primary spellcasting ability. Spell save DCs follow the shared [spell rules](../spells.md#spell-attacks-and-save-dcs). Paladins gain access to 1st-level spells at class level 4, 2nd-level spells at level 7, 3rd-level spells at level 10, and 4th-level spells at level 13.

The list emphasizes protection, healing, courage, and direct opposition to evil, drawing inspiration from the Pathfinder Paladin and D&D 5e Paladin spell lists.

## 1st-Level Paladin Spells

| Spell | School | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bless](../spells/cleric/bless.md) | Enchantment | Choose up to three willing creatures; each gains +1 on attack rolls and saving throws for 1 minute/CL. |
| [Command](../spells/cleric/command.md) | Enchantment | Give a creature a short order; a creature that fails its Will save spends its next turn obeying if able. |
| [Cure Light Wounds](../spells/cleric/cure-wounds.md) | Necromancy | Positive energy restores hit points to one living creature you touch; it damages undead instead. |
| [Detect Magic](../spells/cleric/detect-magic.md) | Divination | Sense active spells within 30 feet and learn each aura's location and school. |
| [Divine Favor](../spells/paladin/divine-favor.md) | Evocation | Gain a Luck bonus on weapon attack and damage rolls for 1 minute. |
| [Protection from Evil](../spells/cleric/protection-from-evil.md) | Abjuration | Ward one creature against a named creature's spells and attacks. |
| [Searing Smite](../spells/paladin/searing-smite.md) | Evocation | Imbue your melee weapon; its next hit deals additional fire damage that scales with caster level. |
| [Shield of Faith](../spells/cleric/shield-of-faith.md) | Abjuration | Grant one willing creature a +2 Deflection bonus to Deflection AC for 1 minute/CL. |

## 2nd-Level Paladin Spells

| Spell | School | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cure Moderate Wounds](../spells/cleric/cure-moderate-wounds.md) | Necromancy | Positive energy restores hit points to one living creature you touch; it damages undead instead. |
| [Enhance Ability](../spells/cleric/enhance-ability.md) | Transmutation | Grant a willing creature a +2 Enhancement bonus to one ability score for 10 minutes/CL. |
| [Hold Person](../spells/cleric/hold-person.md) | Enchantment | A humanoid that fails its Will save becomes Staggered and can repeat the save each turn. |
| [Heroism](../spells/cleric/heroism.md) | Enchantment | Grant a willing creature a +2 Morale bonus on attack rolls, saves, and skill checks for 1 hour/CL. |
| [Lesser Restoration](../spells/cleric/lesser-restoration.md) | Conjuration | End a listed debilitating condition or restore 1d4 temporary ability damage from one score. |
| [Resist Energy](../spells/cleric/resist-energy.md) | Abjuration | Reduce each instance of one chosen elemental damage type for 10 minutes/CL. |
| [Warding Bond](../spells/cleric/warding-bond.md) | Abjuration | Link yourself to a willing creature, improving its defenses while you share its damage. |
