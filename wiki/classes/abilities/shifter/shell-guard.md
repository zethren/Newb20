# Shell Guard

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Turtle form), Tier 2

**Action:** Standard Action.

Until the start of your next turn, you brace inside your shell and gain DR 5/- and a +4 racial bonus to MAD against Trip, Bull Rush, Drag, and Reposition maneuvers. While braced, your speed is 0, you cannot attack, and you cannot take Reactions.
