# Outflank

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +4.

**Benefit:** When you and a conscious ally who also has this feat both threaten the same enemy from opposite sides, you both gain a +2 Teamwork bonus on melee attack rolls against it. This replaces the +1 bonus from [Coordinated Assault](coordinated-assault.md) against that enemy; the bonuses do not stack. When either of you scores a critical hit against the flanked enemy, the other may spend a Reaction to make one melee Attack of Opportunity against it, if it remains within reach and can still be attacked. See [Flanking](../game-mechanics.md#flanking).

**Required for:** None.
