# Raccoondog Form

The Raccoondog form is the Primal Shifter's agile trickster and infiltrator. Tanuki Hengeyokai use this form track. Its form modifiers are added to the [global size modifiers](../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md).

| Tier | Character level | Size | STR | DEX | CON | Natural armor | Form feature |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Medium | +0 | +2 | +0 | +1 | Low-light vision; climb speed 20 ft.; +2 Racial bonus to Stealth |
| 2 | 4 | Large | +2 | +4 | +2 | +2 | [Misdirection](../abilities/shifter/misdirection.md); climb speed 30 ft. |
| 3 | 8 | Large | +2 | +6 | +2 | +3 | [Slip Away](../abilities/shifter/slip-away.md); +2 Racial bonus to Disguise and Investigation |
| 4 | 12 | Large | +2 | +8 | +2 | +4 | [Persistent Shifting](../abilities/shifter/persistent-shifting.md) |
| 5 | 16 | Huge | +4 | +10 | +4 | +5 | [False Double](../abilities/shifter/false-double.md); +2 Racial bonus to Persuasion |
| 6 | 20 | Huge | +4 | +12 | +4 | +7 | [Swift Shifting](../abilities/shifter/swift-shifting.md) |

## Natural Attacks

Damage uses the Raccoondog's current Size column in the [weapon damage by size table](../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size), starting from the Medium base damage below. Slot properties and damage types follow [Combat Scaling](../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#natural-weapon-properties).

| Weapon slot | Medium base damage | Damage types |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Bite (main hand) | 1d4 + 1.5x STR | Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing |
| Claw 1 (off hand) | 1d3 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |
| Claw 2 (off hand) | 1d3 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |

See [Primal Shifter](../primal-shifter.md) and [Shifting Talent](../abilities/shifter/shifting-talent.md) for selection and tier rules.
