# Widen Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +3; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** Double each listed linear dimension of the spell's area of effect. This does not change the spell's range.

**Required for:** None.
