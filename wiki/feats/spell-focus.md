# Spell Focus

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Benefit:** Choose one school of magic. The saving-throw DC of your spells from that school increases by 1.

You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different school.

**Required for:** [Improved Spell Focus](improved-spell-focus.md) for the chosen school.
