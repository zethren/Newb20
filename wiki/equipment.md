# Equipment

## Durability

[Item Durability](game-mechanics/item-durability.md) defines structural hit points, Hardness, the Broken threshold, and the different destruction rules for mundane and magical items.

## Shields and Defensive Gear

- [Defender Combat Feats](feats/defender-combat-feats.md) describes using a shield to protect an adjacent ally.
- [Bodyguard](classes/bodyguard.md) describes the Warden's Shield interaction.
- [Mounted Combat](game-mechanics/mounted-combat.md) describes saddles and locking harnesses, including their effects on a rider who fails a stability check.

## Weapons

- Natural-weapon slots, damage scaling, and damage types are documented in [Primal Shifter](classes/primal-shifter.md).
- Weapon durability and the Broken weapon penalties are documented in [Item Durability](game-mechanics/item-durability.md).

## Transformations

[Transformation Rules](game-mechanics/transformations.md) defines whether equipment is dropped or melds into the transformed body. Only items with the **Shifter** enchantment property remain with characters in Primal Shifter forms or physically coercive transformations such as Baleful Polymorph.

## Healing Kits

[Heal](skills/heal.md) defines the modifiers for damaged, standard, and high-quality kits, healing salves, and herbal remedies.

## Scrolls

The standard purchase price for a scroll is **50 gp × spell level × caster level**. This price includes the parchment, inks, and other materials. Treat a cantrip as a 1st-level spell when calculating its price. The [Wizard](classes/wizard.md#scribe-scroll) rules define scroll crafting. [Use Magic Device](skills/use-magic-device.md#activating-scrolls-and-wands) defines scroll and wand activation.

A general equipment catalog, other item prices, carrying capacity, and crafting or repair rules are not yet documented.
