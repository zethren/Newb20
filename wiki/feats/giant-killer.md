# Giant Killer

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** BAB +1.

You use your low center of gravity and high speed to slide under the guard of massive foes, striking at their vulnerabilities and deforming their footing.

**Benefit:** Against a target at least one size category larger than you, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all Attack Rolls (AB) and Martial Ability Bonus (MAB) checks. For example, a Medium shifter attacking a Large orc or a Large wolf attacking a Huge giant qualifies.

**Required for:** None.
