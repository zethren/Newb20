# Rogue

The Rogue is a mobile, skill-focused class specializing in stealth, precision, and expertise. Rogues excel at finding ways through obstacles and exploiting openings in combat, but are less durable and have narrower armor and weapon training than Fighters.

## Class Features

### Expertise

At 1st level, choose two Rogue class skills. You can take 10 on checks with those skills even when rushed or threatened. At 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th level, choose one additional Rogue class skill to gain this benefit.

### Sneak Attack

When the Rogue hits a Flanked or Flat-Footed target, the Rogue deals the listed Sneak Attack damage. The damage increases by 1d6 at every odd Rogue level after 1st. Sneak Attack applies on every qualifying hit with the Rogue's primary weapon, including iterative attacks. Secondary and off-hand attacks can still trigger strike talents if they deal at least 1 damage after Damage Reduction, but do not add Sneak Attack damage dice. See the shared [Sneak Attack rules](../../../game-mechanics/sneak-attack.md).

A ranged attack can deal Sneak Attack damage only if its target is within 30 feet, unless you have a Rogue Talent that extends this range.

### Trapfinding

At 3rd level, the Rogue can use Perception to find traps and Disable Device to disable them, including magical traps. Trap detection and disabling DCs follow the rules for the particular trap.

## Rogue Talents

You gain one Rogue Talent at every even level from 2nd through 20th. Some talents have a minimum Rogue level or require another talent.

Choose from the [Rogue Talents](talents.md) available to you. Talents can expand Sneak Attack damage, impose debilitating effects, or grant advanced infiltration abilities.
