# Stealth

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Stealth covers avoiding detection through concealment, quiet movement, and concealed manipulation. It combines Hide, Move Silently, and Sleight of Hand.

## Synergy

No cross-skill synergy is proposed yet; Stealth combines distinct forms of concealment and has no established linked check.
