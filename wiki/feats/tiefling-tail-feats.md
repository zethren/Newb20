# Tiefling Tail Feats

These feats provide increasing control over a Tiefling's prehensile tail. Neither feat is granted automatically by the ancestry.

## Prehensile Tail

**Prerequisite:** Tiefling heritage with a prehensile tail.

**Benefit:** You can use your tail as an additional hand to hold, retrieve, and manipulate items that can normally be handled with one hand. You can activate an item with your tail using the item's normal action. Your tail cannot wield a weapon or shield with this feat. The tail does not grant additional actions.

## Tail Combat

**Prerequisite:** Prehensile Tail.

**Benefit:** You can wield a one-handed weapon or a shield with your tail. You must meet the normal requirements to use the weapon or shield; this feat does not grant proficiency. The tail does not grant additional actions or attacks.
