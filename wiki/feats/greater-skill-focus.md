# Greater Skill Focus

**Type:** General Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Skill Focus](skill-focus.md) with the chosen skill; 10 ranks in that skill.

Choose the same skill for which you have Skill Focus. Your Skill Focus bonus with that skill increases to +6 total.

**Required for:** None.
