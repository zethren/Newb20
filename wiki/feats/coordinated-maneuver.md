# Coordinated Maneuver

**Type:** Teamwork Feat.

**Prerequisites:** BAB +1.

When you attempt a combat maneuver against a creature that is within 5 feet of an ally who also has this feat, you gain a +2 Teamwork bonus on the MAB check. The ally must be conscious and able to act. Multiple allies with this feat do not increase the bonus.

**Required for:** None.
