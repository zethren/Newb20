# Disable Device

**Key ability:** Dexterity

Disable Device covers manipulating, disabling, or bypassing mechanical traps and devices. It is distinct from [Open Lock](open-lock.md), which focuses on locks.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Disable Device grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Investigation](investigation.md) checks to examine a mechanism for tampering or concealed features.
