# Improved Spell Focus

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Spell Focus](spell-focus.md) with the chosen school; caster level 4.

**Benefit:** Choose the same school as your Spell Focus. The saving-throw DC of your spells from that school increases by an additional 1, for a total increase of 2.

You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different school for which you have Spell Focus.

**Required for:** [Greater Spell Focus](greater-spell-focus.md) for the chosen school.
