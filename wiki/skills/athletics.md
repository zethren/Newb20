# Athletics

**Key ability:** Strength

Athletics covers strenuous physical tasks, including climbing, jumping, swimming, and using rope. It combines Climb, Jump, Swim, and Use Rope.

Ordinary swimming follows normal d20 Swim rules. Combat-specific swimming checks and recovery while sinking are in the shared [Swimming rules](../game-mechanics/swimming.md).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Athletics grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Acrobatics](acrobatics.md) checks to keep balance or recover from a difficult jump.
