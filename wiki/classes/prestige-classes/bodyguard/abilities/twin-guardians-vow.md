# Twin Guardian's Vow

**Class:** Bodyguard 8

At the start of each day, the Bodyguard can designate a second willing ally as a Protected Charge, in addition to the ally designated through [Guardian's Vow](guardians-vow.md). The Bodyguard can have no more than two Protected Charges at a time.

The Bodyguard's class features that protect a Protected Charge can apply to either or both charges, subject to each feature's normal range and action limits.
