# Practiced Spellcaster

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells from the chosen class.

**Benefit:** Choose one class from which you can cast spells. Your caster level for that class increases by 4, to a maximum equal to your character level. This affects only caster-level-dependent effects and checks. It does not grant access to higher-level spells or increase spell slots, spells known, spells prepared, or class features.

**Required for:** None.
