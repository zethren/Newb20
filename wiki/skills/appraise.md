# Appraise

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Appraise estimates the value, quality, and authenticity of an object by examining its materials, workmanship, and condition.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Appraise grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Investigation](investigation.md) checks to assess an object's value or authenticity.
