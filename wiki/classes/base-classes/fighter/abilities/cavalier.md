# Cavalier

**Class:** Fighter specialization

Cavalier features improve the Fighter's riding, control, and mounted attacks. They apply with any mount that meets the shared [Mounted Combat rules](../../../../game-mechanics/mounted-combat.md); the Cavalier does not gain a bonded mount. These features do not remove or reduce the universal mounted attack and AC penalties.

## Cavalry Training

**Class:** Fighter 4 (Cavalier)

While mounted, you gain a **+2 Supernatural bonus** on Ride checks made to remain mounted or use the Ride Redirection Shield rule in [Mounted Combat](../../../../game-mechanics/mounted-combat.md#61-the-ride-redirection-shield-base-ride-capability).

## Mounted Momentum

**Class:** Fighter 8 (Cavalier)

Once per round, if your mount moves at least 10 feet in a straight line toward a creature before you attack it, your first melee weapon attack that hits that creature before the end of the round deals an extra **1d6 damage**. This movement and attack count as a mounted charge for Cavalier features. This extra damage is not multiplied on a critical hit.

## Sure Seat

**Class:** Fighter 12 (Cavalier)

Whenever you fail a Ride check triggered by your mount attacking, taking damage, or suffering a listed destabilizing condition, you may reroll it once. You must use the new result. This benefit has no per-rest limit.

## Improved Mounted Momentum

**Class:** Fighter 16 (Cavalier)

The extra damage from Mounted Momentum increases to **2d6**.

## Master Cavalier

**Class:** Fighter 20 (Cavalier)

Your Cavalry Training bonus increases to **+4**, and the extra damage from Mounted Momentum increases to **3d6**. When that extra damage is dealt as part of a mounted charge, double it to **6d6**. This doubles only Mounted Momentum's extra damage, not the attack's weapon damage or other bonuses; it is still not multiplied on a critical hit.
