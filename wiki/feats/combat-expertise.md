# Combat Expertise

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 13; BAB +1.

**Benefit:** While fighting defensively, your AC bonus increases by +2. While using Total Defense, your AC bonus increases by +4. These are Circumstance bonuses and apply to the same AC modifier as the underlying defensive option.

**Required for:** [Improved Combat Expertise](improved-combat-expertise.md).
