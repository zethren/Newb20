# Greater Spell Focus

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Improved Spell Focus](improved-spell-focus.md) with the chosen school; caster level 8.

**Benefit:** Choose the same school as your Improved Spell Focus. The saving-throw DC of your spells from that school increases by an additional 1, for a total increase of 3.

You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different school for which you have Improved Spell Focus.

**Required for:** None.
