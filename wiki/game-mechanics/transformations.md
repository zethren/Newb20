# Transformation Rules

While a creature is transformed into another form, treat it as that form's race for racial traits. This applies to Primal Shifter forms, polymorph effects, and Druid Wild Shape.

The transformation suppresses the creature's original ancestry-based racial traits and replaces them with the transformed form's racial package. This includes racial ability bonuses, vision (such as low-light or darkvision), scent and its range, movement speeds, and biological traits such as reproductive capability. Apply the active form's package instead of combining it with the original ancestry's racial package.

The creature retains its base ability scores and nonracial bonuses unless the transformation effect states otherwise. Apply ability-score bonuses according to the [bonus type and stacking rules](../attributes.md#bonus-types-and-stacking); in particular, Size bonuses and racial form bonuses are separate types.

When the transformation ends, the creature's original ancestry-based racial traits return.

## Natural Shifters

A natural shifter changes form through its own supernatural ability rather than through a spell or Polymorph effect. The [Primal Shifter](../classes/base-classes/primal-shifter/index.md#shifting) is a natural shifter. Its forms use the natural-shifter item rule below.

## Hengeyokai Shapechanging

Hengeyokai can assume their natural animal, hybrid, or humanoid form. Their chosen +2 ability bonus and Size persist in every form, but animal-type benefits depend on the form: humanoid form suppresses them, hybrid form grants natural attacks and partial sensory benefits, and natural animal form grants the full animal package.

These form-specific benefits do not grant general polymorph ability-score or natural-armor adjustments. See the [Hengeyokai animal-form profiles](../races.md#hengeyokai-animal-forms) for the exact benefits by type and form.

A Hengeyokai humanoid form may visually resemble any listed base ancestry, but appearance grants none of that ancestry's mechanics. The Hengeyokai's Size and statistics do not change to match the chosen appearance.

In natural animal form, a Hengeyokai cannot speak or manipulate objects with hands. Its hybrid and humanoid forms retain speech and manual dexterity.

## Item and Action Limits

### Natural Shifter Forms

In natural shifter forms such as Primal Shifter forms, the character has no manual dexterity and cannot speak. They cannot drink potions or activate magic items that require verbal or somatic components. Only items with the [Shifter enchantment](magic-item-enchantments.md#universal-item-properties) remain with the character; all other worn or carried items are dropped when the form is assumed.

### Polymorph and Wild Shape

The Druid's [Beast Shape I-VI](../spell-lists/druid.md#3rd-level-spells) spells are Polymorph effects, separate from the Druid's Wild Shape class feature and the Primal Shifter's natural-shifting progression. Under ordinary Polymorph effects and Druid Wild Shape, worn and carried items meld into the transformed body and are inaccessible until the transformation ends. They are not dropped.

### Space and Confines During Polymorph

Before a Polymorph effect resolves, check whether the new form can fit in the available space. If the creature cannot fit and there is no confinement it can break, the spell fails. Otherwise, it makes a Strength check (`1d20 + the new form's Strength modifier, including its Size adjustments`) against the break DC of the confinement. On a failure, the spell fails. On a success, the creature breaks through the confinement; if the new form still cannot fit, the spell fails. The GM sets a break DC when the confinement has no established DC.

Size-changing spells such as Enlarge Animal, Reduce Animal, Enlarge Person, and Reduce Person may specify that worn and carried equipment scales with the creature. That spell-specific rule overrides the ordinary Polymorph equipment rule above.

### Baleful Polymorph and Transformative Curses

Baleful Polymorph and curses that physically change a creature into another form use the natural-shifter item rule: the transformed creature cannot speak or use manual dexterity, and drops all worn or carried items except those with the [Shifter enchantment](magic-item-enchantments.md#universal-item-properties).
