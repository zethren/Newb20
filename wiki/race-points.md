# Ancestry Balance

This maintainer reference records the Race Point (RP) budget used to compare ancestry traits. It is for internal balance work; player-facing ancestry rules are on the [Races](races.md) index and the linked race pages.

## Budget Rules

Each ancestry package, including a base ancestry combined with a planar heritage, uses a 10-RP budget. Retained ability adjustments and Size packages count toward the same budget. Use the listed cost for each ancestry trait.

| Trait benchmark | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one fixed ability score | 2 RP |
| Each +2 Racial bonus to one ability score of the player's choice | 3 RP |
| One additional feat at character creation | 4 RP |
| +1 skill rank per character level | 2 RP |
| +2 Racial bonus to one named skill | 1 RP |
| One additional skill designated as a class skill | 1 RP |
| Weapon familiarity with up to 3 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 1 RP |
| Weapon familiarity with 6 named weapons plus the ancestry-named weapon group | 2 RP |
| Minor racial ability | 1 RP |
| Major racial ability | 3 RP |
| Exceptional or feat-equivalent ability | 4 RP |
| Small Size package, including all listed Size effects | 3 RP net |

Classify non-numeric abilities by their breadth, frequency, level scaling, and combat impact. Ability adjustments use +2 increments; larger adjustments cost the listed amount for each +2 increment. Mechanically meaningful drawbacks may refund points, but the combined refund is capped at 2 RP per ancestry package. Purely narrative limitations do not refund points.

| Half-Orc | 4 RP | 5 RP | Persuasion (1 RP) | 10 RP |

## Balance Questions

- Decide how to allocate the Human ancestry's remaining 1 RP and resolve its Skilled trait cost and Class Skill bonus.
- Set the Human Skilled cost and determine whether it should also grant a Class Skill bonus and a selectable saving-throw bonus.
- Finalize ancestry prices and review Hengeyokai's package and the Shifter Talent feat, including whether its benefit applies only in natural animal form.
- Define Hengeyokai's shared-trait and animal-type package costs; review Tengu Poor Flight against the shared flight penalties.
- Review Turtle, Raccoondog, and Raven form packages alongside Wolf, Feline, and Bear; review the Aasimar and Tiefling combinations.
- Review the scaling value of Human's bonus feat and skill rank; Elf's four-hour Long Rest and weapon familiarity; Dwarf poison immunity and movement; Half-Orc scent; Halfling Size and luck; Half-Elf's overlapping bonuses; Gnome illusion traits; and the weapon-familiarity cost split.
- Confirm whether the Kitsune ancestry's current budget accounts for its three forms and magical tails.
