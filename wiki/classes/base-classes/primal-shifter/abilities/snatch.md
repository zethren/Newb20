# Snatch

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Wolf form), Tier 2

Snatch is a three-stage hold that starts with the Wolf's Bite and continues over the Wolf's turns. Each stage offers a fixed set of actions.

## Stage 1: Snatch

- **Trigger:** Once per round, when a Bite attack hits.
- **Target:** The target gains [Grappled](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#grappled).
- **Same size or larger:** If the target is the same size as the Wolf or larger, the Wolf also gains Grappled.
- **Two size categories smaller:** If the target is two size categories smaller than the Wolf, the target gains [Pinned](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#pinned) instead, and the Wolf gains no condition.
- **One size category smaller:** Not yet defined. See [Open Points](#open-points).

## Stage 2: Hold

While the hold from Stage 1 is active, the Wolf chooses one option:

| Option | Action | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Maintain and advance | Standard action | Maintains the hold and advances a Grappled target to Pinned. Applies primary weapon damage. |
| Break | Free action | Ends the grapple or pin. |
| Swallow | Free action | Requires [Swallow Whole](../../../../feats/swallow-whole.md) and a target two size categories smaller. The target is swallowed. Applies primary weapon damage. |
| Thrash | Move action | Two-size-smaller targets only. Used in place of Stage 3 thrash. |

## Stage 3: Held

This stage applies when the target has not been swallowed.

| Option | Action | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Maintain | Standard action | Maintains the hold. Automatically hits with primary weapon damage. |
| Thrash | Move action | Automatically applies damage for every attack in a full attack, except the first. |
| Break | Free action | Ends the grapple or pin. |

If the target was swallowed in Stage 2, no further Snatch actions apply, except the full-round regurgitate described in [Swallow Whole](../../../../feats/swallow-whole.md).

## Primary Weapon Damage

Primary weapon damage in Stages 2 and 3 uses the Bite. It includes Strength and enchantments, as for any auto-hit attack. Swallowing applies the Swallow Whole damage: primary Bite damage plus the current acid damage, with mythic scaling as normal.

While a hold is active, these attacks against the held target override the primary-weapon lock in [Grapple, Thrash, and Throw](../../../../game-mechanics/grapple-and-throw.md).

## Movement During a Hold

- **Two size categories smaller:** The Wolf may move freely and the target moves with it, in any stage. This does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **Other targets:** The held target may be moved at 1/2 speed.

## Ending a Hold

A hold ends when the Wolf breaks it with a free action, or when the Wolf does not maintain it on its turn.

## Open Points

- The effect against a target one size category smaller is not yet defined.
- It is not yet defined whether Thrash also maintains the hold.
