# Forge Ring

**Type:** Crafting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 3rd-level spells; Craft 8 ranks.

You can create or improve magic rings using the [Magic Item Enchantment rules](../game-mechanics/magic-item-enchantments.md). The item's total enchantment cost cannot exceed its Enchantment Point capacity.

**Required for:** None.
