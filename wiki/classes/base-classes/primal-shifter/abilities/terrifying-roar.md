# Terrifying Roar

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Bear form), Tier 6

**Action:** Free Action.

Terrifying Roar replaces [Demoralizing Roar](demoralizing-roar.md) at Tier 6. Enemies in a 15-foot cone must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half the Shifter level + the Shifter's Constitution modifier) or become [Fear](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#fear) for 1d4 rounds. The Shifter is the source of the Fear.

While Feared, a creature takes a -4 penalty on attack rolls, saves, and skill checks. It cannot attack the Shifter and cannot take any action that moves it closer to the Shifter.
