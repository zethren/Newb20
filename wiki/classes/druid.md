# Druid (Draft)

Druids are Wisdom-based divine spellcasters who protect, study, and embody the natural world. They wield nature magic, travel through difficult terrain, and eventually assume animal forms through Wild Shape.

## Spellcasting

Druids prepare divine spells using Wisdom. Their spell list focuses on nature, weather, healing, summoning, and transformation. The Druid uses the shared full-caster spell-level progression in [Classes](../classes.md#full-spell-level-progression); divine spell slots, preparation limits, and spell DCs remain to be defined.

## Druid Features

- **Druidic:** A private language or body of signs and lore shared by Druids; its actual communication rules need definition.
- **Wild Shape:** Gain the ability to assume animal forms. The level at which it begins, form limits, duration, and whether it uses the shared transformation rules require a dedicated progression.
- **Nature's Stride:** Gain increasing ability to travel through natural terrain and resist environmental hindrances.
- **A Thousand Faces:** At high level, alter appearance through nature magic.

Wild Shape should be its own Druid progression, distinct from Primal Shifter form tracks, and should not grant an ancestry's racial package unless the transformation rules explicitly say so.

## Druid Circle

Choose a circle at character creation. Its features advance by Druid level.

| Circle | Specialty |
| --- | --- |
| Circle of the Land | Terrain-linked magic, recovery, and environmental mastery |
| Circle of the Moon | More capable Wild Shape forms and transformation-focused combat |
| Circle of the Shepherd | Animal communication, summoning, and protection of beasts |
| Circle of the Storm | Weather, wind, lightning, and control of open terrain |

The Circle of the Moon must be balanced against the Primal Shifter; it should not simply reproduce that class's full combat form progression.
