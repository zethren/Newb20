# Lore (Geography)

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Lore (Geography) covers knowledge of regions, terrain, climate, routes, and the features of the natural and settled world.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Lore (Geography) grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Survival](survival.md) checks to navigate or avoid becoming lost.
