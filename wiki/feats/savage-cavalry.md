# Savage Cavalry

**Also known as:** Predator Link.

**Type:** Advanced Animal Feat.

**Prerequisites:** [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md); Ride 5 ranks; BAB +4.

You have trained extensively alongside apex predators, learning how to read the muscle shifts of a wild beast's spine to maintain your lethal edge mid-frenzy.

**Benefit:** You have mastered the extreme turbulence of monstrous mounts. The severe -4 beast-riding penalty is completely and permanently removed, reducing your mounted accuracy and defensive tax to 0.

**Required for:** None.
