# Primal Strike

**Class:** Primal Shifter

Natural weapons gain the following properties for overcoming damage reduction:

| Shifter level | Property |
| ---: | --- |
| 4 | Magic |
| 7 | Cold iron and silver |
| 10 | Alignment-based, matching the character's alignment |
| 16 | Adamantine; also ignores object Hardness |

This progression is independent of the Shifter's form and form tier.
