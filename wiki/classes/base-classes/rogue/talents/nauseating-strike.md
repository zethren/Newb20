# Nauseating Strike

**Class:** Rogue Talent  
**Prerequisite:** Rogue 16, Blinding Strike, Sneak Attack 8d6

When a qualifying hit deals at least 1 damage to the target after Damage Reduction, you can make the target attempt a Fortitude save against your Sneak Attack Talent DC. On a failed save, the target becomes [Nauseated](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#nauseated) for **1 round**.

Each additional failed save from this talent extends the duration by 1 round.
