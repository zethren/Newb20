# Use Magic Device

**Key ability:** Charisma

Use Magic Device represents the ability to activate or operate magic items without relying solely on the user's normal magical training or nature.

## Activating Scrolls and Wands

A caster who knows a spell can activate a scroll containing it without a Use Magic Device check or expending a spell slot. The scroll uses the spell's normal casting time and is consumed when successfully activated. A caster who does not know the spell must succeed at a Use Magic Device check against DC **10 + spell level + caster level**.

To activate a wand, a character must succeed at a Use Magic Device check against DC **15 + spell level + caster level**. Each successful activation expends one wand charge.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Use Magic Device grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Lore (Arcana)](lore-arcana.md) checks to identify or understand a magic item.
