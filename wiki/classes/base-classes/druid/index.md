# Druid

Druids are the most versatile nature-focused spellcasters. They support allies who draw on animals and the natural world, with magic for utility, healing, animal allies, elemental force, and transformation. A Druid's circle develops one of these strengths without replacing the class's broad nature magic.

### Nature's Metal Prohibition

A Druid cannot wield or wear items made primarily of metal, or actively use a metal item as equipment or a tool. Carrying coins or incidental metal objects does not count as using them. This restriction applies in addition to the Druid's armor and shield proficiencies. The [Ironwood](../../../spells/druid/ironwood.md) spell can make wooden equipment as durable as steel without making it metal.

### Alignment

A Druid must have a non-lawful alignment. See [Alignment](../../../alignment.md).

## Spellcasting

Druids prepare divine spells using Wisdom. Their spell list focuses on nature, weather, healing, summoning, and transformation. The Druid uses the shared full-caster spell-level progression in [Classes](../../index.md#full-spell-level-progression). Spell slots, preparation limits, and recovery are class-specific. See the [Druid spell list](../../../spell-lists/druid.md) for available spells and individual spell rules.

## Class Features

### Druidic

At 1st level, you learn Druidic, a private language of speech, signs, and lore shared by Druids.

### Wild Shape

At 1st level, you learn to transform into an animal. You can use Wild Shape twice per Short Rest. Transforming or returning to your normal form is a Standard Action. A transformation lasts up to 1 hour, or until you end it as a Free Action or become unconscious.

Wild Shape is a class feature, separate from the Druid's [Beast Shape I-VI](../../../spell-lists/druid.md#3rd-level-spells) Polymorph spells.

Your ordinary Wild Shape forms are Small or Medium land animals. While transformed, apply the shared [Transformation Rules](../../../game-mechanics/transformations.md): your ancestry traits are replaced by the form's traits, and your worn and carried items meld into the form. You retain your hit points, ability scores, class features, and spellcasting ability. Without the [Natural Spell feat](../../../feats/natural-spell.md), you cannot cast spells while in animal form.

At 4th level, your Wild Shape can assume Tiny forms. At 12th level, you can use Wild Shape three times per Short Rest. Circle of the Moon grants additional form options.

### Nature's Stride

At 2nd level, nonmagical natural undergrowth and similar plant growth do not reduce your movement or prevent you from moving through it.

At 8th level, you and allies within 10 feet of you can move through nonmagical natural undergrowth without reduced movement.

### A Thousand Faces

At 16th level, you can use nature magic to alter your appearance. You can change your apparent age, height, build, and other superficial features, but not your size category, creature type, or game statistics. This is a supernatural effect and does not change your voice, clothing, or equipment.

### Timeless Body

At 18th level, you no longer age and do not suffer age-related penalties. This does not extend your natural lifespan; you still die when you would have reached its natural end.

## Druid Circles

Choose one circle at 1st level. Its features are gained at levels 1, 6, 10, 14, 18, and 20.

| Circle | Focus | Circle feature progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Circle of the Land](circles/land.md) | Utility, travel, and environmental mastery | Terrain affinity; Green Passage; Natural Recovery; Land's Shelter; Master of the Land; Land's Renewal |
| [Circle of the Moon](circles/moon.md) | Expanded Wild Shape and lunar magic | Moon-Touched Forms; Fluid Shifting; Moonbeam; True Form; Lunar Mastery; Archdruid |
| [Circle of the Shepherd](circles/shepherd.md) | Animal communication, companions, and protection | Wild Communion; animal companion; shared senses; guardian beasts; Shepherd of the Wild; Master Shepherd |
| [Circle of the Storm](circles/storm.md) | Elemental wrath and battlefield control | Storm Affinity; Storm's Reach; Elemental Resistance; Tempest's Wrath; Tempest Stride; Storm's Mantle; Avatar of the Storm; Living Tempest |

Circle of the Storm does not gain Wild Shape. Its elemental features replace the Druid's Wild Shape feature and its improvements at levels 1, 4, and 12.
