# Investigation

**Key ability:** Intelligence

Investigation covers deliberately examining a location, object, or record for clues and drawing conclusions from available evidence. It includes Search, Gather Information, Forgery, and Decipher Script.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Investigation grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Perception](perception.md) checks to notice clues while searching a specific area.
