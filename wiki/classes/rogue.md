# Rogue (Draft)

The Rogue is a mobile, skill-focused class specializing in stealth, precision, and expertise. Rogues excel at finding ways through obstacles and exploiting openings in combat, but are less durable and have narrower armor and weapon training than Fighters.

## Subclasses

- **Assassin:** Ambushes, poison, and lethal precision; gains reliable tactical access to targets, not unrestricted instant kills.
- **Burglar:** Infiltration, traps, escape, and misdirection; improves entry, extraction, and battlefield control.
- **Swashbuckler:** Agile dueling, parries, ripostes, and movement around opponents.
