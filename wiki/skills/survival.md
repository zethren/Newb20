# Survival

**Key ability:** Wisdom

Survival covers finding a route, tracking, identifying immediate environmental dangers, and sustaining oneself in the wild. Active scent tracking uses Survival as described in [Scent](../game-mechanics/scent.md).

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Survival grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Perception](perception.md) checks to notice tracks or other signs in the outdoors.
