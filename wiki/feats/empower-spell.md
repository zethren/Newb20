# Empower Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +2; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** When you cast a spell, you may increase all its variable numerical effects by 50%. Roll each variable effect normally, then multiply the result by 1.5 and round down.

**Required for:** None.
