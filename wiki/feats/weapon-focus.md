# Weapon Focus

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Proficiency with the chosen weapon, or the ability to use the chosen natural attack; BAB +1.

Choose one specific weapon. You gain a +1 Supernatural bonus on attack rolls made with that weapon.

**Required for:** [Greater Weapon Focus](greater-weapon-focus.md).
