# Turtle Form

The Turtle form is the Primal Shifter's aquatic grappler and shell-armored defender. Kappa Hengeyokai use this form track. Its form modifiers are added to the [global size modifiers](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md).

| Tier | Character level | Size | STR | DEX | CON | Natural armor | Form feature |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Medium | +0 | +0 | +2 | +2 | Swim speed 30 ft. |
| 2 | 4 | Large | +2 | +0 | +4 | +4 | [Shell Guard](../abilities/shell-guard.md); swim speed 40 ft. |
| 3 | 8 | Large | +2 | -2 | +6 | +6 | [Undertow](../abilities/undertow.md) |
| 4 | 12 | Large | +2 | -2 | +8 | +8 | [Persistent Shifting](../abilities/persistent-shifting.md) |
| 5 | 16 | Huge | +4 | -4 | +12 | +10 | [Shell Fortress](../abilities/shell-fortress.md); swim speed 50 ft. |
| 6 | 20 | Huge | +4 | -4 | +16 | +12 | [Swift Shifting](../abilities/swift-shifting.md) |

## Natural Attacks

Damage uses the Turtle's current Size column in the [weapon damage by size table](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#weapon-damage-by-size), starting from the Medium base damage below. Slot properties and damage types follow [Combat Scaling](../../../../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#natural-weapon-properties).

| Weapon slot | Medium base damage | Damage types |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Bite (main hand) | 1d4 + 1.5x STR | Blunt, piercing, and slashing |
| Claw 1 (off hand) | 1d3 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |
| Claw 2 (off hand) | 1d3 + 0.5x STR | Piercing and slashing |

See [Primal Shifter](../index.md) and [Shifting Talent](../abilities/shifting-talent.md) for selection and tier rules.
