# Bear's Maneuvering Stance

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Bear form), Tier 3

The Bear gains a +6 racial bonus to MAD against Trip, Bull Rush, Drag, and Reposition maneuvers.

This is a class feature, distinct from the [Stand Ground combat feat](../../../../feats/stand-ground.md).

**Related:** [Bear Form](../forms/bear.md)
