# Warden's Watch

**Class:** Bodyguard 2

While a Protected Charge is adjacent (within 5 feet), the Bodyguard absorbs half of the damage that charge takes from physical attacks, spells, or environmental hazards. The charge takes the other half. This applies separately to each Protected Charge within 5 feet, so the Bodyguard can protect both at once. If the Bodyguard and one or more charges are caught in one area effect, the Bodyguard takes their own damage normally once, plus the intercepted half of each charge's damage.

This feature does not apply to a charge more than 5 feet away.
