# Kitsune Magical Tails

A Kitsune's tails manifest as it grows in power. At 1st character level, it has one magical tail; it gains one additional tail at each listed character level:

| Character level | Total tails |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 |
| 5 | 2 |
| 7 | 3 |
| 9 | 4 |
| 11 | 5 |
| 13 | 6 |
| 15 | 7 |
| 17 | 8 |
| 20 | 9 |

Whenever a tail is gained, choose one effect from the [Ioun Stone effects](../game-mechanics/magic-item-enchantments.md#ioun-stones). That tail grants its chosen effect as a supernatural ability; it does not require crafting, Enchantment Points, or an item slot. The tail's effect otherwise uses the Ioun Stone's rules, including its bonus type and duration. A magical tail and an Ioun Stone with the same effect stack. Effects from multiple tails follow the normal Ioun Stone stacking rules.

These are ancestry features, not selectable feats.
