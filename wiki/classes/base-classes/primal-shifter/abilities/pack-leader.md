# Pack Leader

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Wolf form), Tier 5

While the Wolf is grappling or has pinned a creature, each ally gains +2 AB and +2 MAB against that creature, whether or not it is flanked. This extends [Pack Tactics](pack-tactics.md) from flanking to the Wolf's grapple and pin.

**Open:** Whether this bonus stacks with Pack Tactics when the target is both flanked and grappled or pinned. Whether allies must be within a set range of the Wolf or the target.
