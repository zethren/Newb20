# Lethal Precision

**Class:** Rogue Talent  
**Prerequisite:** Rogue 4, Sneak Attack 2d6

Your Sneak Attack deals an additional **1d6 precision damage** on every qualifying hit made with your designated Primary Weapon. This damage is added to each hit's Sneak Attack damage dice; it does not apply to secondary or off-hand attacks.
