# Cleric (Draft)

Clerics are divine spellcasters who serve a deity, sacred cause, or spiritual tradition. They combine prepared divine magic with martial training and choose a domain at character creation.

## Spellcasting

The Cleric prepares divine spells and uses Wisdom for spellcasting. A Cleric's deity or tradition determines which spells and domains are available. The Cleric uses the shared full-caster spell-level progression in [Classes](../classes.md#full-spell-level-progression); divine spell slots, preparation limits, and spell DCs remain to be defined.

## Divine Domain

Choose one domain at character creation. A domain grants a thematic ability at level 1, domain spells or expanded spell access, and additional features at later Cleric levels. Domain features should arrive across the progression rather than front-loading the whole subclass at level 1.

| Domain | Specialty |
| --- | --- |
| Life | Healing, recovery, and protection from death |
| War | Weapon use, courage, and battle magic |
| Knowledge | Lore, languages, investigation, and divination |
| Nature | Beasts, plants, and the natural world |
| Protection | Wards, resistance, and defending allies |
| Trickery | Deception, concealment, and misdirection |
| Light | Radiance, revelation, and opposing darkness |
| Death | Necromancy, endings, and control of the boundary between life and death |

The deity/domain relationship, domain spell lists, and whether a Cleric receives one or more domains remain open.

## Core Feature Roadmap

Divine Domain, Channel Divinity or equivalent, and Divine Intervention are candidate features. Define the exact scope and uses of channeling with the conditions, healing, and undead rules.
