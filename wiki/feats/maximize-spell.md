# Maximize Spell

**Type:** Spellcasting Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Ability to cast spells.

**Spell-level adjustment:** +3; see [Metamagic](../spells.md#metamagic).

**Benefit:** All variable numerical effects of the spell take their maximum possible value instead of being rolled.

**Required for:** None.
