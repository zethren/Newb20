# Gnome

Gnomes are Small. Apply the Small adjustments and effects from [Combat Scaling](../game-mechanics/combat-scaling.md#size-modifiers), including its ability, attack, AC, maneuver, Stealth, reach, movement, weapon-damage, ration, weight, and space rules.

- **Intelligent:** +2 Racial bonus to Intelligence.
- **Darkvision:** See in darkness out to 60 ft.
- **Illusion Affinity:** +1 Circumstance bonus to the spell DC of Illusion spells the Gnome casts.
- **Illusion Resistance:** +2 Racial bonus on saving throws against Illusion effects.
