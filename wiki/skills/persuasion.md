# Persuasion

**Key ability:** Charisma

Persuasion covers changing attitudes and influencing others through argument, negotiation, deception, or intimidation. It combines Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate.

Persuasion checks normally use Charisma. [Intimidating Presence](../feats/intimidating-presence.md) allows Strength to replace Charisma on checks made to intimidate; it does not apply to other uses of Persuasion.

## Synergy

At 5 ranks, Persuasion grants a +2 Synergy bonus to [Disguise](disguise.md) checks to maintain a false identity through conversation.
