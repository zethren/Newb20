# Zen Archery

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisite:** Wisdom 13.

**Benefit:** You may use your Wisdom modifier instead of your Dexterity modifier on ranged weapon attack rolls. This changes only the modifier used for the attack roll; it does not change damage or apply to spell attacks.

**Required for:** None.
