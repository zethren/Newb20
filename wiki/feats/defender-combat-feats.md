## 1. Core Defender Combat Feats

### Stand Ground (Combat Feat)
You transform your threatened area into a hard kinetic denial zone, physically stopping enemies from slipping past you to reach your allies.
*   **Prerequisites:** Base Attack Bonus **+3**.
*   **The Mechanic:** When an enemy creature provokes an Attack of Opportunity from you by moving out of or through your threatened grid squares, you can choose to execute a **Martial Ability Check (MAB)** vs. the target's static **Martial Ability Defense (MAD)** in place of a standard damage strike.
*   **Success State (Stopped Cold):** If your MAB check meets or exceeds the target's MAD, you successfully intercept their momentum. The target takes 0 damage, but **their base movement speed is instantly reduced to 0 feet** for the remainder of their turn, locking them in place exactly where you struck them.

### Vanguard Intercept (Combat Feat)
You watch your ally's flank like a hawk, physically throwing your body into the path of incoming danger to absorb a blow meant for a friend.
*   **Prerequisites:** Dexterity 13, Base Attack Bonus **+2**.
*   **Action Cost:** Reaction (executed in response to an enemy launching a physical melee or ranged attack against an adjacent ally).
*   **The Check:** Roll `d20 + MAB` against the attacking enemy's MAD.
*   **Success:** You and your ally instantly **swap 5-foot grid squares**, and the incoming enemy attack roll is re-routed to resolve against your defenses (including your full armor and shield values) instead of your ally's.
*   **Failure:** You do not swap squares or redirect the attack; it resolves against its original target.

### Raise Shield Other (Combat Feat)
You tilt your shield outward, creating a mobile canopy that extends your physical blocking surface to protect a nearby comrade.
*   **Prerequisites:** Shield Proficiency, Base Attack Bonus **+1**.
*   **Action Cost:** Move Action.
*   **The Baseline Mechanic:** While you are actively wielding a light shield, heavy shield, or tower shield, you can spend a move action to lock your guard onto a single chosen ally who is currently **adjacent to you (within 5 feet)**.
*   **The Benefit:** Until the start of your next turn, as long as that ally remains within 5 feet of your character, **they gain a circumstance bonus to their Armor Class (AC) equal to the Shield AC bonus of your equipped shield**. While maintaining this guard, you lose your shield's AC bonus to your own defenses as your cover is turned outward.
