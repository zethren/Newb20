# Blinding Strike

**Class:** Rogue Talent  
**Prerequisite:** Rogue 12, Disorienting Strike, Sneak Attack 6d6

When a qualifying hit deals at least 1 damage to the target after Damage Reduction, you can make the target attempt a Fortitude save against your Sneak Attack Talent DC. On a failed save, the target becomes [Blinded](../../../../game-mechanics/conditions.md#blinded) for **1 round**.

Each additional failed save from this talent extends the duration by 1 round.
