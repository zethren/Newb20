# Slip Away

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raccoondog form), Tier 3

**Action:** Reaction, when a melee attack misses you.

Move up to 10 feet without provoking an attack of opportunity from the attacker. This movement must end in an unoccupied space. You gain a +2 AB bonus on your next attack against that attacker before the end of your next turn.
