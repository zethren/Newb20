# Human

- **Flexible Ability:** Choose one ability score. You gain a +2 Racial bonus to it.
- **Bonus Feat:** Gain one additional feat at character creation. You must meet its prerequisites.
- **Skilled:** Gain 1 additional skill rank per character level, subject to the normal maximum ranks per skill.
