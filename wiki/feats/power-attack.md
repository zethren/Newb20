# Power Attack

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Strength 13; BAB +1.

**Benefit:** Before making a melee attack, choose a number from 1 to your BAB. Starting with that attack, you take a penalty to all melee attack rolls equal to the chosen number until the start of your next turn. Each successful hit during that time gains +1 AD for each point of penalty; a melee attack made with a weapon held in two hands instead gains +2 AD for each point of penalty.

**Required for:** [Cleave](cleave.md).
