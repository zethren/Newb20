# Greater Two-Weapon Fighting

**Type:** Combat Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 19; BAB +11; [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](improved-two-weapon-fighting.md).

When you make a Full Attack Action while wielding a weapon in each hand, you gain one additional off-hand attack at BAB -5. The off-hand attack granted by Two-Weapon Fighting remains at your highest BAB with no penalty.

**Required for:** None.
