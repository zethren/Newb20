# Prehensile Tail

**Prerequisite:** Tiefling heritage with a prehensile tail.

**Benefit:** You can use your tail as an additional hand to hold, retrieve, and manipulate items that can normally be handled with one hand. You can activate an item with your tail using the item's normal action. Your tail cannot wield a weapon or shield with this feat. The tail does not grant additional actions.

**Required for:** [Tail Combat](tail-combat.md).
