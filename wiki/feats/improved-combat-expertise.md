# Improved Combat Expertise

**Type:** Martial Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Combat Expertise; BAB +4.

**Benefit:** While fighting defensively, reduce the attack-roll penalty by 2 (from -4 to -2). While using Total Defense, reduce the attack-roll penalty by 4 (from -8 to -4).

**Required for:** None.
