# Craft Wondrous Item

**Type:** Crafting Feat.

**Prerequisites:** Ability to cast 2nd-level spells; Craft 5 ranks.

You can create or improve magic clothing, boots, amulets, gloves, and helmets using the [Magic Item Enchantment rules](../game-mechanics/magic-item-enchantments.md). The item's total enchantment cost cannot exceed its Enchantment Point capacity.

**Required for:** [Craft Ioun Stone](craft-ioun-stone.md).
