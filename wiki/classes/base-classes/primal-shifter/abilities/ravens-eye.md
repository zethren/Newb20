# Raven's Eye

**Class:** Primal Shifter (Raven form), Tier 3

**Action:** Move Action.

Choose one creature you can see within 60 feet. Until the start of your next turn, you and your allies gain a +2 AB bonus on the first attack each of you makes against that creature, provided the attacker can see it.
